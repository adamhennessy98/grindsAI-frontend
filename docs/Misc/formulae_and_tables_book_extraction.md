## Page 4 — Length and Area

### Parallelogram
- Perimeter = 2a + 2b  
- Area = ah = ab sin C

### Circle / Disc
- Length of circle (circumference) = 2πr  
- Area of disc = πr²

### Triangle
- Perimeter = a + b + c  
- Area = ½ah = ½ab sin C = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)]  
  where s = (a + b + c)/2

## Page 5 — Surface Area and Volume

### Cylinder
- Curved surface area = 2πrh  
- Volume = πr²h

### Cone
- Curved surface area = πrl  
- Volume = ⅓πr²h

### Sphere
- Surface area = 4πr²  
- Volume = (4/3)πr³

### Frustum of a Cone
- Volume = ⅓πh(R² + Rr + r²)

### Solid of Uniform Cross-Section (Prism)
- Volume = Ah, where A is the area of the base

### Pyramid on Any Base
- Volume = ⅓Ah, where A is the area of the base

## Page 6 — Area Approximations

### Trapezoidal Rule
Area ≈ (h/2)[y₁ + 2y₂ + 2y₃ + ... + 2yₙ₋₁ + yₙ]

or equivalently:  
Area ≈ (h/2)[first + last + twice the rest]

### Simpson's Rule
Area ≈ (h/3)[y₁ + 4y₂ + 2y₃ + 4y₄ + ... + 2yₙ₋₂ + 4yₙ₋₁ + yₙ]  
*(where n is odd)*

or equivalently:  
Area ≈ (h/3)[first + last + twice the odds + four times the evens]

## Page 8 — Trigonometry (Compound and Double Angle Formulae)

### Compound Angle Formulae

- cos(A + B) = cos A cos B − sin A sin B  
- cos(A − B) = cos A cos B + sin A sin B  
- sin(A + B) = sin A cos B + cos A sin B  
- sin(A − B) = sin A cos B − cos A sin B  
- tan(A + B) = (tan A + tan B) / (1 − tan A tan B)  
- tan(A − B) = (tan A − tan B) / (1 + tan A tan B)

### Double Angle Formulae

- cos 2A = cos²A − sin²A  
- sin 2A = 2 sin A cos A  
- tan 2A = 2 tan A / (1 − tan²A)  
- cos 2A = (1 − tan²A) / (1 + tan²A)  
- sin 2A = 2 tan A / (1 + tan²A)  
- cos²A = ½(1 + cos 2A)  
- sin²A = ½(1 − cos 2A)

## Page 9 — Trigonometry (Products, Sums, Triangle)

### Products to Sums and Differences

- 2 cos A cos B = cos(A + B) + cos(A − B)  
- 2 sin A cos B = sin(A + B) + sin(A − B)  
- 2 sin A sin B = cos(A − B) − cos(A + B)  
- 2 cos A sin B = sin(A + B) − sin(A − B)

### Sums and Differences to Products

- cos A + cos B = 2 cos((A+B)/2) cos((A−B)/2)  
- cos A − cos B = −2 sin((A+B)/2) sin((A−B)/2)  
- sin A + sin B = 2 sin((A+B)/2) cos((A−B)/2)  
- sin A − sin B = 2 cos((A+B)/2) sin((A−B)/2)

### Trigonometry of the Triangle

**Sine rule:**  
a/sin A = b/sin B = c/sin C

**Cosine rule:**  
a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos A

**In a right-angled triangle:**
- sin = opposite / hypotenuse  
- cos = adjacent / hypotenuse  
- tan = opposite / adjacent

**Area of triangle:**  
Area = ½ab sin C

## Page 10 — Co-ordinate Geometry: Line

### Line Formulae

- **Slope:** m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)  
- **Distance:** d = √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]  
- **Midpoint:** ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2)  
- **Equation of a line:** y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)  or  y = mx + c  
- **Area of triangle with one vertex at origin:** ½|x₁y₂ − x₂y₁|  
- **Point dividing a line segment in ratio a:b:**  
  ((bx₁ + ax₂)/(a + b), (by₁ + ay₂)/(a + b))  
- **Distance from point (x₁, y₁) to line ax + by + c = 0:**  
  |ax₁ + by₁ + c| / √(a² + b²)

## Page 11 — Co-ordinate Geometry: Circle; Geometry and Vectors

### Circle

- **Equation of circle, centre (h, k), radius r:**  
  (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r²

- **Circle x² + y² + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0** has:  
  centre (−g, −f) and radius = √(g² + f² − c)

- **Tangent to circle at point (x₁, y₁):**  
  (x − h)(x₁ − h) + (y − k)(y₁ − k) = r²  
  or  xx₁ + yy₁ + g(x + x₁) + f(y + y₁) + c = 0

### Geometry — Notation

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| AB | Line through A and B |
| [AB] | Line segment from A to B |
| \|AB\| | Distance from A to B |
| AB⃗ | Vector from A to B |
| a = OA⃗ | Vector from origin O to A |

### Vector Operations

- v₁ = x₁**i** + y₁**j**,  v₂ = x₂**i** + y₂**j**  
- **Scalar product:** v₁ · v₂ = x₁x₂ + y₁y₂ = |v₁||v₂| cos θ  
- **Norm:** |v| = √(x² + y²)

## Page 16 — Financial Mathematics

*In all of the following, t is the time in years and i is the annual rate of interest, depreciation or growth, expressed as a decimal (e.g. i = 0·08 represents 8%).*

### Key Formulae

**Compound interest:**  
F = P(1 + i)ᵗ  
*(F = final value, P = principal)*

**Present value:**  
P = F / (1 + i)ᵗ  
*(F = final value, P = present value)*

**Depreciation — reducing balance method:**  
F = P(1 − i)ᵗ  
*(P = initial value, F = later value)*

**Depreciation — straight line method:**  
Annual depreciation = (initial cost − residual value) / useful economic life

**Amortisation (mortgages and loans — equal repayments at equal intervals):**  
A = R × [(1 − (1 + i)⁻ᵗ) / i]  
*(R = repayment amount, A = amount advanced)*

## Page 17 — Financial Mathematics: APR

### Annual Percentage Rate (APR) — Statutory Formula

The APR is the value of i (expressed as a percentage) such that:

Σ Aₖ / (1 + i)^Tₖ  =  Σ Rⱼ / (1 + i)^tⱼ

where:
- M = number of advances  
- Aₖ = amount of advance k  
- Tₖ = time in years to advance k  
- m = number of repayments  
- Rⱼ = amount of repayment j  
- tⱼ = time in years to instalment j

### Converting Period Rate to APR/AER

i = (1 + r)ᵐ − 1

where:
- i = APR or AER (as a decimal)  
- r = period rate (as a decimal)  
- m = number of periods in one year

## Page 18 — Statistics and Probability

### Mean

**From list:** μ = Σx / n

**From frequency table:** μ = Σfx / Σf

### Standard Deviation

**From list:** σ = √[Σ(x − μ)² / n]

**From frequency table:** σ = √[Σf(x − μ)² / Σf]

### Sampling

- The sample mean x̄ is an unbiased estimator of the population mean μ.  
- The adjusted sample standard deviation s = √[Σ(x − μ)² / (n − 1)] is an unbiased estimator of σ.  
- Standard error of the mean: σ/√n  
- Standard error of the proportion: √(pq/n)

### Hypothesis Testing

**Tukey quick test:**

| Significance level | 5% | 1% | 0·1% |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical value of tail-count | 7 | 10 | 13 |

**t-test:**  
t = (X̄ − μ) / (s/√n)

**χ²-test:**  
χ² = Σ (Oᵢ − Eᵢ)² / Eᵢ

## Page 36 — Mechanics: Linear Motion

### Linear Motion with Constant Acceleration

- v = u + at  
- s = ut + ½at²  
- v² = u² + 2as  
- average speed = distance / time  
- s = ((u + v) / 2) × t

### Relative Motion

- s_bc = s_b − s_c  
- v_bc = v_b − v_c  
- a_abc = a_ab − a_ac

### Momentum and Impulse

- Momentum of a particle: mv  
- Impulse (change in momentum): mv − mu

### Newton's Experimental Law (NEL)

v₁ − v₂ = −e(u₁ − u₂)

### Conservation of Momentum

m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂

## Page 37 — Mechanics: Circular Motion and Gravitation

### Circular / Angular Motion

- Angle in radians: θ = s/r  
- Angular velocity: ω = θ/t  
- Linear velocity and angular velocity: v = rω  
- Centripetal acceleration: a = rω² = v²/r  
- Centripetal force: F = mrω² = mv²/r

### Gravitation

- Newton's law of gravitation: F = Gm₁m₂/d²  
- Force and acceleration: F = ma  
- Weight: W = mg = Vρg  
- Acceleration due to gravity: g = GM/R²  
- Period of a satellite: T² = 4π²R³ / GM

### Moments

- Moment of a force: M = Fd  
- Torque of a couple: T = Fd
