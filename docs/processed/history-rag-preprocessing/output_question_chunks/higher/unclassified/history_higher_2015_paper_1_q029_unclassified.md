        (a) Document A contains much more opinion than facts. It is a fact to say that
          thousands will be visiting Dublin in the following year, but the rest of the
            extract is dominated by opinion. The writer seeks to embarrass Cosgrave
          through the use of rhetorical questions to point out how un-Christian it is
           not to re-house the poor working class.

               It could be argued that his claim that there are “miles of squalid
          tenements” and “acres of slum courts” is an exaggeration, but it is
          probably factual. The example he quotes of overcrowding may be
           exceptional, but possibly true.

               It is the writer’s opinion that if we cannot provide proper accommodation
