                                            Ireland: Topic 6
          Government, economy and society in the Republic of Ireland, 1949‐1989



Case study to which the documents relate:
The First Programme for Economic Expansion, 1958‐1963

Study the documents opposite and answer the questions below.

1.    (a)   According to document A, why were Whitaker and others able to ‘do something
            positive’ for Ireland?
      (b)   In document A, how did Whitaker and his colleagues perform their work during the
           period that followed?
       (c)   In document B, when was the Programme presented to the Dáil?
      (d)   In document B, with what policy did the Programme replace the policy of self‐
            sufficiency?
      (e)   According to document B, why was the Programme successful?
