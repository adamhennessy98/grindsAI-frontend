                          Politics and society in Northern Ireland, 1949-1993

                              A (30 marks)
                     Recommended maximum time: 10 minutes

In this edited extract a British paratrooper recalls patrolling the countryside of South Armagh by
helicopter. Read it and answer the questions which follow.

            I check my equipment: map, compass, rifle, ammunition and, lastly, I check with my radio
operator to see whether he has everything.
       Within a few seconds we are in the air and moving along at low level, dodging power lines
and telephone lines.
    We are moving down toward the village of Cullaville, right on the border with the Republic.
The helicopter swings round in a tight turn, drops and touches down. As soon as we touch ground,
we are off and running, guns at fire positions.

Source: Based on material in Keith Jeffery (ed.), The Divided Province: the Troubles in Northern
Ireland, 1969-1985 (London: Orbis Publishing Ltd., 1985).
