Ireland: Topic 5
                           Politics and society in Northern Ireland, 1949‐1993

                               A (30 marks)
                      Recommended maximum time: 10 minutes

In this edited extract John Hume reflects on the background to his role in the ‘University for Derry’
campaign. Read it and answer the questions which follow.

      Our growing sense of isolation in Derry was enhanced by the closure of our rail links to
Donegal in 1953 and to Dublin in 1965 and the subsequent downgrading of the link to Belfast.
      The final straw was the recommendation, in 1965, that a new university be constructed in the
small neighbouring town of Coleraine, rather than the city of Derry, which already had the historical
building of Magee College, the obvious site for a new seat of learning.


Source: Britain and Ireland: Lives Entwined III, (The British Council, 2002).
