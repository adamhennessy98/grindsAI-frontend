                             A (30 marks)
                     Recommended maximum time: 10 minutes

This edited extract refers to an Irish neighbourhood in Victorian Glasgow, 1861. Read it and
answer the questions which follow.

    Down towards the river there is a blaze of lights from street lamps, shop windows, open
doors and the upper-storey windows of tenements.
     Both pavements are crowded. Crowds are also flowing up the centre of the street,
occasionally pushed aside by cabs, fish-barrows and donkey-carts laden with coal. Street vendors
are everywhere, selling fish, oranges, cheese, handkerchiefs, braces and razors.
    An old blind fiddler is playing ‘Rory O’More’ and a couple of ballad singers are performing an
Irish patriotic song.

Source:  J. E. Handley, The Irish in Modern Scotland (Cork: Cork University Press, 1947).
