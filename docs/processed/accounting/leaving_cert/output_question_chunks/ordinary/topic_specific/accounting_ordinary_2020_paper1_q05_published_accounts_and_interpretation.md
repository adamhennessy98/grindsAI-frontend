(c)   Acid Test Ratio: 1.64 : 1                                                                [10]
      No. This ratio tells us that for every €1 they owe they have liquid assets of €1.64. This is
       better than the recommended ratio of 1 : 1.

(d)   Return on Capital Employed:                                                          [10]

       Net profit + interest       100        53,500 + 12,000        100        7.71%
                           ×         =                   ×            =
           Capital employed         1            849,500            1

     Return on capital employed has decreased from 10% in 2018 to 7.71% in 2019. This is a
     decrease of 2.29%. This is a good return, the business is profitable, and you would only get a
     return of about 1%/2% from a bank or risk free investment.
