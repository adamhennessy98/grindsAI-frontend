(a)
   •  The circuits in a computer's processor are made up of transistors. A transistor is a switch
       that is activated by the electronic signals it receives. Signals can be 0V or 5V. The digits 1
      and 0 used in binary reflect the on and off states of a transistor.
    •  As there are only two possible states, binary is more reliable than any other number
       systems.
