# Marking Scheme

Question 8                                                             5 marks

     Cost (advantage)
       No need to for one-off up-front capital costs.
       No need for upgrading/replacements costs.
     Cost (disadvantage)
       Costs are ongoing (pay-as-you-go) which could work out more expensive in the long
         run.
       Ongoing costs to cloud service provider to keep software and hardware up-to-date

     Environment (advantage)
       Economy of scale meaning that cloud providers can offer shared services more
          efficiently thereby reducing carbon footprint.
       Many cloud companies generate their own energy (and supply to grid)
       Less paper.
     Environment (disadvantage)
       Owing to its success there is a greater demand for cloud services – thus increasing the
        energy demand and greater carbon footprint.

    Award 3 marks for better of cost or environment as follows:
         2 marks for better of advantage/disadvantage as follows:
