(iii)                                                        4 (2, 2) marks

   Option 1: Waterfall/Staged
     Highly structured, follows a sequence of phases e.g. analysis, design, implementation,
      deployment and maintenance.
     Each phase must be completed before moving to the next, ensuring a structured and
       predictable process.
     Comprehensive documentation such as requirements specifications, design plans, test
       cases.
     Suitable for large projects when requirements are unlikely to change.

   Option 2: Agile/Iterative
     Less structured/more flexible, breaks project into smaller iterations called sprints.
     Functional components are delivered incrementally.
     Customer collaboration throughout the project lifecycle.
     Very responsive to dynamic/changing requirements.

           For each feature:
