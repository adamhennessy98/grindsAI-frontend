# Question

Question 5

There are three different ticket prices available for attending a concert. A full price adult ticket
costs €20. Children under 16 receive a 20% discount. Anyone aged 16 to 21, who is also a student,
receive a 10% discount.

The flowchart for this algorithm is shown in Figure 1 below with some information missing.





                                       Figure 1




Select the appropriate letter from the flowchart and enter it in Column B in the table below to
match it to the correct statement in Column A.

                 Column A                           Column B
                  Statement                      Flowchart Letter (A, B, C or D)

                    PRINT(price)


             Is age < 21 AND student = “yes”?


             price = price – (20% of price)


             price = price – (10% of price)
