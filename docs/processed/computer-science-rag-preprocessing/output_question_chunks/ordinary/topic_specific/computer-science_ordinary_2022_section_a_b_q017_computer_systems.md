# Question

Question 12

In a game, shown in Figure 8, a player begins on the Start square, facing towards square 1. They
must follow the instructions given to them and see what number square they end up on. There are
only three instructions in the game. Instructions for the game are provided in pseudocode.


    Instruction             Explanation

     Step         Step forward one square
   Forward

                     Rotate 90 degrees to the
 Turn Right                  right
                    (Do not move forward)
                                                                         Figure 8
                   Rotate 90 degrees to the left
 Turn Left
                    (Do not move forward)


(a)  What number square will the player finish on when they follow these instructions?

 Step Forward                Answer:
 Turn Right
 Repeat 2 times:
     {Step Forward}


(b)  What number square will the player finish on when they follow these instructions?

 Repeat 2 times:             Answer:
     {Step Forward}
 Repeat 3 times:
     {Turn Right
     Step Forward}
