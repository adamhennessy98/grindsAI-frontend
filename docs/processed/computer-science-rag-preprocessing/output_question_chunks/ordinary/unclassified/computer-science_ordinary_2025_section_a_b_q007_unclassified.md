(b)
      Simplicity for Hardware: Computers use electrical circuits, which have two states: on (1)
      and off (0).
      Reliability: Using just two states (1 and 0) reduces the chance of errors or
       misinterpretations.
      Efficiency in Processing: The binary system allows computers to perform operations and
       calculations much faster because it only needs to handle two states.
     Compatibility with Logic: Binary aligns with Boolean logic (true/false), which is the
      foundation of programming and decision-making in computers.
