# Question

Question 7

Figure 3 shows the wave signals for analogue and digital inputs. Describe one difference between
the two types of input.

# Marking Scheme

Question 7                                                         5 marks
Digital input signals are used to represent items that only have two (2) states, such as ON
(binary 1) or OFF (binary 0) states. Analog signals are variable, they have multiple states. Analog
input signals can represent such items as temperature or light levels, etc.
