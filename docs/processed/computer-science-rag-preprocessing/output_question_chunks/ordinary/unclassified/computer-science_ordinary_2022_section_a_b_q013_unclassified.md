# Question

Question 8

COVID-19 has had a significant impact on the use of technology and the Internet for remote work
in business and education. It has also highlighted issues in the area known as the “digital divide”.

(a)   Describe one way digital technology has helped us during the COVID-19 Pandemic.





(b)   Explain what is meant by the term “digital divide”.
