# Question

6.   Ordnance Survey Map

     Examine the 1:50 000 Ordnance Survey map and legend accompanying this paper and
     answer each of the following questions.

        (i)   Name the antiquity at S 226 248.




         (ii)   Give a six-figure grid reference for the Garda Station in Clonmel.




         (iii)  Measure the length, in kilometres, of the R688 regional road from where it enters the
        map at S 183 270 to where it meets the N24 at S 198 236.




       (iv)  Estimate the area, in square kilometres, of the land north of the N24.
