12A.  Population Dynamics


                                Rural population (% of total population)

                            Region         1960         2015

                             Ireland           54%         37%

                         Japan            37%         7%

                          United States       30%         18%


                                                                Amended from World Bank



      Examine the data above showing rural population as a percentage of the total population in
       selected regions in 1960 and 2015.

           (i)     Using graph paper, draw a suitable graph to illustrate this data.
                                                                                  [16m]
                                Bar Chart                   Pie Chart
        Title                    2 marks                 2 marks
        Vertical axis named        1 mark                  1 + 1 mark (Circle & Centred)
       Horizontal axis named      1 mark
      6 items illustrated         2 marks each graded (2/1/0) 2 marks each graded (2/1/0)


      Accept any suitable graph / chart.
      Naming of graph / chart type is not sufficient for title marks. The title must have reference to
           chart content and it can be written anywhere on the graph.
        If graph paper is not used deduct 2 marks from total.
      Apply the 2 marks graded if the numbered spaces and the column widths are not plotted
            accurately.





           (ii)     Explain briefly one reason for the trend in rural population indicated in the table above.

12B.   Migration

      Examine how ethnic and religious issues can arise as a result of migration.
                                                                                  [30m]

       Ethnic issue identified            2 marks
       Religious issue identified          2 marks
      Examination                    13 x SRPs

         All further issues identified require examination.
        Credit a named example of where issues arise for 1 x SRP from examination.
       Max 6 x SRP’s if only ethnic or religious issues examined.
        Discussion without reference to migration max 2 x SRP’s.
        Discussion without reference to ethnic or religious issues max 2 x SRP’s.
       Accept a positive or negative interpretation of issues.




12C.  Urban settlement

      Answer the following question using evidence from the aerial photograph OR from the 1:50 000
      Ordnance Survey map and legend to support your answer. Do not refer to both in your answer.

       Explain three reasons why the town of Macroom developed at its present location.
                                                                                  [30m]

      Three reasons @ 10 marks each
       For each reason:
      Reason stated                        2 marks
        Aerial photograph OR OS map evidence    2 marks
       Examination                          3 x SRP’s

      Accept a max of two reasons on different modes of transport.
        If both the Ordnance Survey map and the aerial photograph are used mark separately and
         award the highest mark.
