# Question

8.    Aerial Photograph and Ordnance Survey Map

     Examine the aerial photograph of Foxford and the 1:50 000 Ordnance Survey map and
     legend accompanying this paper and answer each of the following questions.

        (i)   Name the road that enters the aerial photograph in the left foreground.




         (ii)   Give a six-figure grid reference for the bridge that is located in the centre
          middleground of the aerial photograph.




         (iii)   In what direction was the camera pointed when the aerial photograph was taken?



       (iv)   Indicate whether the following statement is true or false by ticking () the correct box.

          There is a church in the shape of a cross in the left foreground.

               True             False
