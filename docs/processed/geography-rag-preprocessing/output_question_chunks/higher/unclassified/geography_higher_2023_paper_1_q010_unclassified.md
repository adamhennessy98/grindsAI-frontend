# Question

8.    Aerial Photograph and Ordnance Survey Map

     Examine the aerial photograph of Cavan and the 1:50 000 Ordnance Survey map and legend
     accompanying this paper and answer each of the following questions.

        (i)    In what direction was the camera pointed when the aerial photograph was taken?




         (ii)   Give a four-figure grid reference for the lake that is located in the right foreground of
          the aerial photograph.




         (iii)  Which of the following regional roads can be seen in the left background of the aerial
          photograph? Tick () the correct box.

           R 212          R 188             R 198            R 165

       (iv)   State the type of aerial photography used for the aerial photograph accompanying this
           paper.
