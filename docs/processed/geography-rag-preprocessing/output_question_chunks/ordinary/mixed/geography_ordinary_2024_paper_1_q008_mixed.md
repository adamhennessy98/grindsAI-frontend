     answer each of the following questions.

        (i)   The town of Bantry is located in which area of the Ordnance Survey map?
            Tick () the correct box.

               South west

               South east

         (ii)  What is the length of the R584 road from where it enters the map at W 043 560 to
         where it meets the N71 road at W 005 533?
