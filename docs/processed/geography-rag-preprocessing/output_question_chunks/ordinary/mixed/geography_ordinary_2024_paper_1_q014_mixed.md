# Question

12.  Graphical Interpretation





     Examine the infographic above on housing in Ireland in 2022 and answer each of the
      following questions.

        (i)   Of the owner-occupied homes, were there more with a loan or mortgage or more
          without a loan or mortgage?




         (ii)  What was the average weekly rent (in €) paid in Donegal?




         (iii)  How many homes in Ireland had solar panels?




       (iv)  Which county council had the highest number of homes with solar panels?




       (v)   State one example of renewable energy, other than solar.
