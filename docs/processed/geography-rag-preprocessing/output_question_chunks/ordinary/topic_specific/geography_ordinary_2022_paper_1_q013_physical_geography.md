# Question

11.  Trade Statistics

                         Ireland’s Export Partners in 2017, 2018 and 2019
                                      Percentage of Irish Exports

                     Country        2017     2018      2019

                  USA            27       28        31

                 UK             12       11        10

                     Belgium         11       13        10

                   Germany         8        7         9

                      Netherlands      5        5         6

                      China            4        3         5

                       Switzerland       5        5         4

                     Others          28       28        25

                                                  Amended from CSO

     Examine the table above showing the percentage share of exports received by Ireland’s
     export partners in 2017, 2018 and 2019 and answer each of the following questions.

        (i)   What percentage of Irish exports went to Belgium in 2018?




         (ii)  Which country received 9% of Irish exports in 2019?




         (iii)   Calculate the difference in the percentage of Irish exports received by the USA in 2017
         and 2019.




       (iv)  Name one country listed above that received a lower percentage of Irish exports in
         2019 than they did in 2017.




       (v)  Name one product that is exported from Ireland.
