# Question

5.    Ordnance Survey Map


      Examine the 1:50 000 Ordnance Survey map and legend accompanying this paper and
      answer each of the following questions.

           (i)    What is the length of the R587 regional road, from where it enters the map at
       W 285 650 to where it meets the R584 regional road at W 296 703.
               Tick the correct box.

              4.1 km

              6.7 km



           (ii)       If a person travelled from the school in Dromleigh W 284 652 to the
            Garda Station in Macroom town, in what direction would they have travelled?
               Tick the correct box.

             North

              North-east
