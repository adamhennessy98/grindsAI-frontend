# Question

11.   Statistical Interpretation

                   Area of Forested Land in Selected EU Countries in 2020

                            Area of Forested Land      % of Land
            EU Country
                             (thousand hectares)     Covered by Forestry

            Sweden               27,980                  63

              Spain                 18,572                  37

            Germany              11,468                  32

              Poland                 9,464                  30

                Ireland                799                   11

            Denmark              628                   15

                                                                 Amended from ec.europa.eu

     Examine the table above showing information on the area of forested land (thousand
      hectares) and the percentage of land covered by forestry in selected EU countries in 2020
     and answer each of the following questions.

        (i)   What was the area of forested land (thousand hectares) in Spain?




         (ii)  Which country had 32% of its land covered by forestry?




         (iii)  Which country had the largest area of forested land?




       (iv)  Which country had the smallest percentage of its land covered by forestry?




       (v)   Suggest one benefit to a country of having more of its land covered by forestry.
