# Question

questions. Some of the table has been completed for you.

        (i)   Match each of the letters A, B, C and D with the feature that best matches it in the
           table below.

         (ii)   Indicate, by ticking the correct box, whether each of the features named in the table is
         most associated with the processes of erosion or the processes of deposition.

# Marking Scheme

Questions 1 to 3

                                        All questions carry 100 marks.


Question 1

1A.  Ordnance Survey Map

     Examine the 1:50 000 Ordnance Survey map and legend accompanying this paper.

    Draw a sketch map of the area shown on the Ordnance Survey map.

    On it, show and label each of the following:

        •  Lough Cullin
        •   Griffins Island
        •  An area of mixed woodland
        •  The entire area of land above 200 metres in the north-east of the map
        •  The entire course of the Clydagh River.


     Sketch Outline:

     Limits/Frame                        1m

     Proportions (portrait)                   2m

      Overall Impression                    2m graded (2/1/0)

                                                        Sub-total 5m

      Five items shown and labelled as follows:

     Showing                           3m graded (3/1/0)

     Labelled                           2m

                                                        Sub-total 25m

     For an obvious tracing allow 50% of the marks obtained for showing and labelling items
     and 0m out of 5m for limits, proportion and overall impression.

                                                                                     Total 30m
