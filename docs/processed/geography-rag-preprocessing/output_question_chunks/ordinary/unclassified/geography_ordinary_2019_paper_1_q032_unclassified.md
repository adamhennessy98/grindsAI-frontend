# Question

Question 1

1A.   Ordnance Survey Map

      Examine the 1:50 000 Ordnance Survey map and legend accompanying this paper.

     Draw a sketch map of the area shown on the Ordnance Survey map.

     On it, show and label each of the following:

            •   The highest point on the Ordnance Survey map
            •   Templemore Lake
            •   The River Suir from S 132 728 to where it leaves the map at S 119 680
            •  An area of coniferous planation in the south east
            •  An area of deciduous woodland south of Templemore.
                                                                            [30m]



1B.   Landform Development

      Answer (i) or (ii).

           (i)     Explain, with the aid of diagrams, the formation of any two landforms.

                                      Or

           (ii)    Describe and explain any two processes of mass movement.
                                                                            [40m]

1C.   Human Interaction with the Rock Cycle

       Describe and explain how humans interact with the rock cycle with reference to
      one of the following:

          •  Quarrying
          •  Mining
          •  Oil/Gas exploitation
          •  Geothermal energy production.
                                                                            [30m]
