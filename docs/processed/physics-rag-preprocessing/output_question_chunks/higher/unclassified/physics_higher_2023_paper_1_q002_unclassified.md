# Question

1.    In an experiment to determine the acceleration due to gravity g, the time t for an object to fall
     from rest through a distance s was measured. The procedure was repeated for a series of values
      of s.

     The following data were recorded.

       s (cm)          40         50         60         70         80         90

        t (cs)           29         32         35         38         40         43

        (i)   Draw a labelled diagram of the apparatus used in the experiment.

         (ii)   Indicate the distance s on your diagram.

         (iii)  Describe how the time interval t was measured.                                         (18)

       (iv)  Draw a suitable graph to show the relationship between s and t.

       (v)   Use the graph to calculate a value for g.                                                  (18)

       (vi)  The object used was a smooth metal sphere. Explain why.                                   (4)
