      Light of wavelength 450 nm is incident on a metal that has a work function of 2.4 eV.
         (ii)   Calculate the maximum speed of the emitted electrons.
           hf = Φ + ½mv2
           c = fλ or f = (3 × 108)/(450 ×10–9) = 6.67 × 1014 [Hz]
      Φ = (2.4)(1.6 ×10–19)= 3.84 ×10–19 [J]
          v = 3.56 ×105 m s–1                                                      [6 + 3 + 3 + 3]
        It is observed that as the wavelength of the incident light increases, the speed of the emitted

         (iii)   Explain these observations.
