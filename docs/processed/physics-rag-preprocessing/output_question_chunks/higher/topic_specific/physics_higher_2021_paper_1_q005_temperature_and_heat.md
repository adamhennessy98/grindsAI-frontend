# Question

4.   A student measured the resistance R of a wire of length 30 cm at different values of temperature .

     The following data were recorded.

       (°C)        0        20       40       60       80       100

            R (Ω)        5.35       5.60       5.85       6.04       6.28       6.51

        (i)   Draw a labelled diagram of how the apparatus was arranged in this experiment.

         (ii)  How did the student make the temperature of the wire 0 °C?                            (15)
         (iii)  Draw a suitable graph to show the relationship between R and .

       (iv)  Use your graph to determine the temperature when the resistance is 6 Ω.                (12)


     The student measured the diameter of the wire to be 2.4 mm.

       (v)  How did the student measure the diameter of the wire?

       (vi)   Calculate the resistivity of the metal at a temperature of 20 °C.                          (13)
