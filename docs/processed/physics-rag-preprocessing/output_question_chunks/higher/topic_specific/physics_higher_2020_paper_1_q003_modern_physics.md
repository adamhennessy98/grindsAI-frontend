# Question

2.    In an experiment to determine the refractive index of glass, light was passed through a glass block
     and the angles of incidence i and refraction r were measured for different values of i.
     The following data were recorded.


                                         i (°)           30     40     50     60     70     80

                           r (°)          19     25     31     35     39     41


         (i)    Explain how the refracted ray and the angle of refraction were determined.
           (A labelled diagram may help your answer.)

         (ii)  Why would using a smaller angle of incidence have led to a less accurate measurement of the
           angle of refraction?                                                                        (15)

         (iii)  Use the data to draw a suitable graph to verify Snell’s law.

        (iv)   Explain how your graph verifies Snell’s law.

       (v)   Use your graph to calculate the refractive index of the glass.                               (25)
