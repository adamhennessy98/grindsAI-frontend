# Question

2.   A student carried out an experiment to measure the focal length of a concave mirror.

        (i)   Draw a labelled diagram of the apparatus used in this experiment.                 (12)
         (ii)   State the formula used to calculate the focal length.
         (iii)  On your diagram, indicate and label the measurements taken by the student.
       (iv)  What instrument was used to take these measurements?                          (18)
       (v)  Why did the student measure the approximate focal length at the start of the
          experiment?
       (vi)  The image distance is the less accurate measurement. Explain why.                (10)
