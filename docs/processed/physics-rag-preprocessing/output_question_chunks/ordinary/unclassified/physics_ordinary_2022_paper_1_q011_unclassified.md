        (i)   What is meant by reflection?

         (ii)   In your answerbook, copy and complete the ray diagram below to show how a
          magnified image is formed in a concave mirror.

         (iii)  The image formed is real. Explain what is meant by a real image.                  (18)

     The object is 20 cm in front of the concave mirror. The mirror has a focal length of 12 cm.

       (iv)   Calculate the position of the real image formed.

       (v)   The object has a height of 4 cm. Calculate the height of the image.                 (18)
