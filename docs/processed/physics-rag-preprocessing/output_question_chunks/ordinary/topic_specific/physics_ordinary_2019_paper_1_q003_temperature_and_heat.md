# Question

2.   A student carried out an experiment to measure the specific latent heat of fusion of ice.
     During the experiment, ice at 0 °C was added to a calorimeter containing warm water.

        (i)   Draw a labelled diagram of the apparatus used in this experiment.                 (12)

         (ii)   The ice was crushed before it was added to the water. Why was the ice crushed?    (6)

         (iii)  Mass and temperature were measured in this experiment.
        Name (a) one mass measured and (b) one temperature measured.                 (12)

       (iv)  Name an instrument used to measure one of the quantities listed in (iii) above.      (6)

       (v)   State one precaution used in this experiment, other than crushing the ice.            (4)
