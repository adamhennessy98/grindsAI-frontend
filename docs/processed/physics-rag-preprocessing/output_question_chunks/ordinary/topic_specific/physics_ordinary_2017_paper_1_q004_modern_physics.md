# Question

3.   An experiment was set up to measure the focal length of a concave mirror.
     The table shows the data recorded during the experiment.

                      u (cm)       15       25       45

                         v (cm)       30       17       13



        (i)   Draw a labelled diagram of the apparatus used in this experiment.             (12)

         (ii)  How did the observer know that the apparatus was correctly arranged
           to record the data?                                                                   (6)

         (iii)   Indicate on your diagram the measurements that were taken.                    (6)

       (iv)   Calculate the value of the focal length f of the mirror, using the data above.    (12)

       (v)  Why might it be an advantage to use a darkened room when carrying out
             this experiment?                                                                      (4)
