# Question

6.   Answer any eight of the following parts (a), (b), (c), etc.

      (a)  What is meant by the moment of a force?

      (b)  A car accelerates from a speed of 17 m s–1
           to a speed of 28 m s–1 in a time of 8 s.
           Calculate the acceleration of the car.

       (c)   Convert 30 °C into kelvin (K).

      (d)  Which of the following is the unit of work?

                    joule               watt                farad             metre

       (e)  Name an instrument used to measure voltage.

        (f)   An object has a height of 2 cm. Its image in a mirror has a height of 5 cm.
           Calculate the object’s magnification.

      (g)   Explain the difference between heat and temperature.

      (h)  What are complementary colours of light?

        (i)   Describe how infrared radiation can be detected.

        (j)   What is meant by capacitance?

       (k)   Describe how white light can be separated into its constituent colours.

        (l)    State what each letter in Einstein’s famous equation E = mc2 stands for.
