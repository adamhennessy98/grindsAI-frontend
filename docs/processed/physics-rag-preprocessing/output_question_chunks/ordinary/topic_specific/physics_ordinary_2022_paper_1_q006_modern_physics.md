# Question

5.    In an experiment to verify Joule’s law, a constant current I was passed through a heating coil
     immersed in water and the rise in temperature Δθ was recorded. This procedure was
     repeated for a number of different currents. The mass of the water and the length of time
      for which the current was flowing were both kept constant.

     The student recorded the following results.

        I (A)           1           1.5         2           2.5         3           3.5

        I2 (A2)

     Δθ (°C)          1.2          2.7          4.8          7.5         10.8        14.7


        (i)   Draw a labelled diagram of the apparatus used in this experiment.

         (ii)  Why were the mass and the time kept constant?

         (iii)   In your answerbook, copy and complete the table above.                          (18)

       (iv)  Use all of the data to plot a graph of I2 against Δθ.

       (v)   Use your graph to find the current that caused a change in temperature of 6 °C.

       (vi)   Explain how your graph verifies Joule’s law.                                        (22)
