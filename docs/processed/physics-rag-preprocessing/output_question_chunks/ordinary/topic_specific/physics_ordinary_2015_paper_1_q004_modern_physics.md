# Question

3.    In an experiment to measure the focal length of a concave mirror, a student placed an object
      in front of the mirror so that a real image was formed. The student repeated the experiment by
      placing the object at different positions.

     The table shows the data recorded by the student.

                   u (cm)        20      25      30      60

                     v (cm)        60      38      30      19.5


       (i)   Draw a labelled diagram showing the arrangement of the apparatus used.           (12)
        (ii)  How was the position of the image located?                                           (6)
        (iii)  Show the distances u and v on your diagram.                                          (6)
       (iv)  Calculate the value of f, the focal length of the mirror.                             (12)
      (v)  Why did the student repeat the experiment?                                           (4)
