# Question

4.   A student carried out an experiment to measure c, the specific heat capacity of water.
    He added heat energy to water in a copper calorimeter.

     The following results were recorded.

       Mass of empty copper calorimeter                        = 0.0745 kg

       Mass of calorimeter and cold water                       = 0.1498 kg

           Initial temperature of cold water                          = 18 °C

         Final temperature of water                              = 23 °C

       Heat energy added                                     = 1703 J

        (i)   Draw a labelled diagram of the apparatus used in this experiment.

         (ii)  How did the student supply the heat energy?                                      (12)

         (iii)   Calculate the mass of the water.

       (iv)   Calculate the increase in temperature of the calorimeter and cold water.           (12)

       (v)   State the formula used to calculate the heat gained by a material as it changes
          temperature.

       (vi)  Use your answers for (iii), (iv) and (v) to calculate c, the specific heat capacity of water.

     Note: Heat energy added = Heat energy gained by water + calorimeter.                  (16)

                               (specific heat capacity of copper = 390 J kg–1 K–1)
