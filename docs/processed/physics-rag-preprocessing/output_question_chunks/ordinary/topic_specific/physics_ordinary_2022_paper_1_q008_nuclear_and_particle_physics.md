# Question

6.   Answer any eight of the following parts (a), (b), (c), etc.

      (a)   State Newton’s first law of motion.

      (b)  A boy applies a force of 20 N to pull his sleigh for 150 m. Calculate the work done
          by the boy.

       (c)  What is the difference between heat and temperature?

      (d)  Draw a labelled diagram to show how light travels through an optical fibre.

       (e)   Describe how to charge an electroscope.

        (f)   Two resistors of resistance 4 Ω and 7 Ω are connected in series.
           Calculate the combined resistance of the two resistors.

      (g)  When the frequency of a sound wave increases, its pitch also increases. What is
          observed to happen to a sound when its amplitude increases?

      (h)   Describe how to show the magnetic field of a bar magnet.                                         N             S

        (i)   Name the three primary colours of light.

        (j)   What is meant by nuclear fission?

       (k)  A fuse is a safety device used in an electrical plug. Describe how a fuse works.

        (l)    Explain what is meant by the half‐life of a radioactive sample.                     (8 × 7)
