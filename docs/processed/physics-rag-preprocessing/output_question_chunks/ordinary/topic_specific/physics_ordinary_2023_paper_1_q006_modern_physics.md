# Question

5.    In an experiment to determine the resistivity of a nichrome wire, a student measured the
      resistance R and the length l of the wire. She repeated this for different lengths of wire.
     She also measured the diameter d of the wire.

     The student recorded the following results.

          l (cm)         20         30         40         50         60         70

     R (Ω)           0.08        0.12        0.17        0.21        0.25        0.29

     d (mm)                                   2

        (i)   Name the apparatus used in this experiment to measure (a) the length of the wire
             (b) the resistance of the wire and (c) the diameter of the wire.

         (ii)  How did the student check that the wire had the same diameter throughout its length?
                                                                                               (15)

         (iii)  Use the data in the table to draw a graph on graph paper of length l against
           resistance R.                                                                       (13)

       (iv)   State the relationship between l and R.

       (v)   State the formula used to calculate ρ, the resistivity of the wire.                    (12)
