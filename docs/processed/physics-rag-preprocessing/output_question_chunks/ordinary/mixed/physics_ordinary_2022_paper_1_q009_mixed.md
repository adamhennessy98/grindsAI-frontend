# Question

7.   A train of mass 420000 kg started from rest and
     accelerated to a velocity of 25 m s–1 in a time of 6
     minutes.

        (i)   What is meant by velocity?

         (ii)   Convert 6 minutes into seconds.

         (iii)   Calculate the acceleration of the train.
           Include units in your answer.

       (iv)   Calculate the force required to accelerate the train.

       (v)   Calculate the distance the train travelled in 6 minutes.                             (30)

     The train then maintained this speed of 25 m s–1 for a further 15 minutes.

       (vi)   Calculate the distance the train travelled during this 15 minute interval.

        (vii)  Draw a labelled diagram to show the forces acting on the train while it is moving with
           constant speed.

        (viii) An object may have a constant speed but not a constant velocity. Explain why.

       (ix)  Draw a speed‐time graph for the train during the first 21 minutes of its journey.    (26)
