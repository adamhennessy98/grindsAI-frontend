# Question

Question 2 - Answer 2(a) and 2(b)

2(a) The image shows a ‘Modpool’, which is a portable swimming pool made from upcycled
      shipping containers.

        (i)    Explain what is meant by the term upcycling.

        (ii)   Customers who purchase a product such as a
         Modpool expect that the product is of high quality.

           Suggest two ways in which the manufacturer could
          ensure that each Modpool produced is of a high quality.


2(b) A Modpool has been installed at a home, as shown below. The shipping container used to
    make the Modpool is manufactured from steel.

                                               (i)   Give two reasons why steel is a suitable material for the
                                 manufacture of shipping containers.

                                                 (ii)   Suggest two ways the safety of this Modpool could be
                                enhanced for users.

                                                  (iii)  An electronic circuit is required to activate a siren (buzzer)
                            when the water level in the swimming pool is too high.

                              Draw a labelled diagram of a suitable circuit to activate the
               Modpool              siren.


Answer 2(c) or 2(d)


2(c)  The manufacturers of the Modpool allow their customers to design their perfect swimming
     pool using Computer-Aided Design (CAD) software.

        (i)      Briefly outline two constraints when designing a suitable Modpool for a home.

        (ii)    Give two benefits of using CAD when designing products.


                           OR


2(d) The heater, jets and lights of a swimming pool can all be controlled from a phone using an
     app.

        (i)   Outline two advantages of being able to use a phone to
           control these features.

        (ii)   Suggest one additional feature, other than those listed
          above, that could be controlled using the app.
