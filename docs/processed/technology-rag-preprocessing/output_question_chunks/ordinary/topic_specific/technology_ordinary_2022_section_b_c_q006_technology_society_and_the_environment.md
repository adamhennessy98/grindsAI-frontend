# Question

Question 3 - Answer 3(a) and 3(b)

3(a)  Swytch are a London-based company who convert standard bicycles into E-Bikes using
     a high-torque brushless motor.

        (i)   Give two benefits of converting a standard bicycle
            into an E-bike.

        (ii)   Briefly describe what is meant by the term torque
        when referring to motors.




3(b) The 1.5 kg battery pack can power the modified bicycle for up to 50 km and is usually stored
      in a holder at the front of the bicycle.

        (i)  Name a suitable material for the manufacture of
          the battery pack holder, shown.

        (ii)   Suggest two reasons why your chosen material is
            suitable for the manufacture of the battery pack holder.

         (iii)  Describe, using notes and sketches, how the
           battery pack holder could be attached to the front
           of the bicycle.                                                                                  Battery pack holder
