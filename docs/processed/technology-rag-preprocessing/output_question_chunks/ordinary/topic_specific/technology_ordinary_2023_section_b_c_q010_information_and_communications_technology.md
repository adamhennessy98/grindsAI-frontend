Option 3 - Information and Communications Technology - Answer 3(a) and 3(b)

      3(a)  Video conferencing platforms have become an essential part of the Irish education system in
           recent years. Many schools use these platforms so that learning continues, and connections
           are maintained among students, parents, and educators.

                (i)  Name two video conferencing platforms.

                 (ii)   Outline one advantage and one disadvantage of using
                video conferencing in education.



      3(b) Network topography describes how computers can be connected together. The two most
        common methods are star topography and ring topography.


                                           (i)   Using notes and annotated sketches, describe the difference
                             between a star topography and a ring topography.

                                            (ii)   Describe two advantages of networking a computer system.

                                              (iii)  Outline two benefits of using a tablet device over a desktop
                              computer in the workplace.


    Answer 3(c) or 3(d)



      3(c)  The internet has become one of the key drivers of social evolution.  It has changed business,
         consumer habits, and even the way criminals operate online.

          Cybercrimes carried out by criminals sometimes use Cryptocurrency.


                (i)    Briefly outline one example of a cybercrime.

                 (ii)   Describe what you understand by the term Cryptocurrency.


                              OR



      3(d)  Fibre optic cables and copper cables can be used for transferring data.


                                                  (i)   Outline two benefits of using fibre optic cable instead of
                                   copper cable for data transfer.

                                                    (ii)  Describe what is meant by the term encryption when referring
                                      to data storage.
