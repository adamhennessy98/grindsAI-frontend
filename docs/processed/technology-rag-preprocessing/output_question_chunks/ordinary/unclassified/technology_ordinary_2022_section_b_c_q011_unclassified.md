Option 4 - Manufacturing Systems - Answer 4(a) and 4(b)

4(a) When a company produces a product, it must be packaged securely for
      distribution, storage, and sale.


        (i)   The packaging, as shown in the image, has been mass-produced.
           Describe what is meant by the term mass-produced.

        (ii)   Suggest two ways in which a company could be more environmentally
           conscious when considering the choice of packaging for their product.


4(b) The blockage of the Suez Canal by the grounded container vessel, the Ever Given, had a
      significant impact on the global supply of products in 2021.  It particularly affected
     companies which use a Just-In-Time (JIT) system when sourcing materials and components
      for their products.

                                             (i)    Explain the manufacturing term Just-In-Time.

                                               (ii)   Suggest one advantage and one disadvantage of using a
                                     Just-In-Time system of manufacturing.

                                                (iii)   Briefly describe three unintended costs for manufacturers as
                                a result of the disruption to the supply chain of products in
                                  2021.
