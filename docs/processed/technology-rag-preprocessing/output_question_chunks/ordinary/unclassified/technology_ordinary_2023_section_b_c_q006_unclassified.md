# Question

Question 3 - Answer 3(a) and 3(b)

3(a) The image shows the logo for the Leinster Model Flying Club.
        It is one of the longest running model flying clubs in Ireland.

        (i)   Give two advantages of having a logo for a club.

        (ii)   Suggest a software package that could be used to design the logo.



3(b) The image shows a Hobbyzone Carbon Cub plane and controller.
        It contains a 2200 mAh, 11 V lithium battery and is
     charged using a USB power source.


        (i)   Describe what is meant by a 2200 mAh battery and
          by a USB power source when referring to the
            specifications of the model plane.

        (ii)   Calculate the power delivered when the motor draws 2200 mA
          from the 11 V battery.      Note: P = V × I

         (iii)  Describe, using a simple circuit diagram, the difference between batteries connected in
            series and batteries connected in parallel.


Answer 3(c) or 3(d)



3(c)  Floats, like those shown in the image below, can be attached to the model aeroplane for
      taking off and landing on water.


                                             (i)  Suggest a suitable material for the manufacture of the floats.
                                        Justify your selection.

                                               (ii)  Describe, using notes and sketches, how the floats could be
                                 attached to the main body of the aeroplane.


                           OR


3(d) Aerodynamics is a key consideration when designing an aeroplane.


        (i)   Describe what is meant by the term aerodynamics when designing an aeroplane.

        (ii)   Large design and build projects use planning tools
          such as Gantt charts.
          Give two reasons for the use of planning tools
             in large projects.
