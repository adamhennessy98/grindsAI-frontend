# Marking Scheme

5.   An elevator with mass of 2000 kg is raised 15 meters in 20 seconds.
     The elevator has a factor of safety of 5 in its design specification.

        (i)    Calculate the work done and the power required to raise the elevator.
         (Assume g = 9.81 m/s2)


        Calculation:
      work = force x distance where force = 2000 kg x 9.81 m/s² = 19620 N
      work = force x distance = 19620 N x 15 m = 294300 J

      power required = work / time = 294300 J / 20 s = 14715 W = 14.7 kW.


        (ii)   Explain what is meant by the term factor of safety of 5.

        A factor of safety of 5 means that the system is designed to withstand loads or
           stresses that are five times greater than the maximum expected load or stress.
