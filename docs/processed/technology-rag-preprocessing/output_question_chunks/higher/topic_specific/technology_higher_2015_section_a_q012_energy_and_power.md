# Marking Scheme

8.   (i)  A study lamp is connected to a 12 volt power supply and is drawing a current of 3 amps.
           Calculate the power of the lamp.

            Calculation:

              Power = Current × Voltage  =   3 × 12 =  36 watts



      (ii)  An electrical heating element having a resistance of 24Ω is connected to a 240V supply for a
          period of 12 hours. Calculate the cost of the energy used if electrical energy costs 25 cents per kWh.

                                        Calculation:
                                        Current =  Voltage   =  240  =  10Amps
                                                      Resistance      24
                                     Power =  Current × Voltage   = 10 × 240 = 2.4kW
                                      Energy cost =  2.4 × 12 × 0.25 =  €7.20
