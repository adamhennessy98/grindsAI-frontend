# Marking Scheme

5.     (i)  A circuit requires a 5 V supply. Find a value of R2 in order to deliver a 5 V supply at A.

     Vout = (R2/R1+R2) x Vin
     Vout         =     (R2/120+R2)x6 = 5                          6 V
      Therefore R2  =     600 Ω
       Alt:    Voltage divider rule: The voltage drop across both resistors               R1 = 120 Ω
          when combined is 6V. Drop in voltage across 120 Ω = 1 V,
            drop in voltage across R2 = 5 V, therefore R2 = 120 Ω x 5 =600 Ω
