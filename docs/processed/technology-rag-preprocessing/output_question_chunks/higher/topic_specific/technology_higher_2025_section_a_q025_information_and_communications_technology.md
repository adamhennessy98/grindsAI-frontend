# Marking Scheme

14.   Cutting fluids and lubrication systems are used to ensure the safety and long-life of CNC
      machines.
         (i)    Name two machine lubricants.
            Mineral or synthetic oils, grease, graphite, etc.

         (ii)    Describe how cooling and lubrication will prolong the life of cutting tools.
             Lubricants are substances that reduce friction and offer smooth movement
           between the running parts of machinery and equipment. Cutting and cooling
               fluids will reduce the build-up of heat which will prolong tool life.
