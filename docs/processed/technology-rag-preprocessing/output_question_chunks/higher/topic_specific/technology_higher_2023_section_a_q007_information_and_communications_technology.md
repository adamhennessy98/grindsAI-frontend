# Marking Scheme

1.    In 2021, five ‘Robot City Trees’ were installed in Cork city
      centre, the trees use IoT technology and natural moss to
     reduce air pollution in the surrounding areas.

        (i)    Explain the term IoT.

           IoT - Internet of Things/devices that connect and
          exchange data over the internet.

        (ii)   Outline one other method of reducing air pollution in
          our cities.

           Planting significant numbers of new trees, using electric vehicles, reducing, or
           eliminating sources of pollution e.g., diesel/petrol vehicles, use public transport etc.
