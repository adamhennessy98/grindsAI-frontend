# Marking Scheme

14.  Vacuum forming machines can be used to form intricate hollow thermoplastic objects.

        (i)   Outline two safety hazards to be aware of when
           using a vacuum forming machine.

           Risk of burns, the plastic sheet is heated to form
          the shape.
         The sheets need to be locked into place as the
         vacuum pump could cause the sheets to collapse.
          Fumes/smells from heated plastic, clamping of
            fingers etc.



        (ii)   Describe one safety feature integrated into a vacuum forming machine.

          Heat is regulated by a thermostatic control which turn off the heater.
          Timer/alarm to alert you when required heating time has elapsed.
