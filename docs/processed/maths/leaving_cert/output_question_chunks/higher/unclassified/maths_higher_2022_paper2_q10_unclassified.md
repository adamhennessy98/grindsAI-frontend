Section B              Contexts and Applications           100 marks

Question 10                                                                      (50 marks)
(a)   In an athletics competition, there were a number of heats of the 1500 m race.
      In the heats, the times that it took the runners to complete the 1500 m were approximately
     normally distributed, with a mean time of 225 seconds and a standard deviation of
    12 seconds.
        (i)   Find the percentage of runners in these heats who took more than 240 seconds to run
          the 1500 m.





        (ii)  The 20% of runners with the fastest times qualified for the final.
          Assuming the race times were normally distributed as described above, work out the
          time needed to qualify for the final, correct to the nearest second.





(b)   Sally takes part in a number of different races in the competition.
     The probability that she makes a false start in any given race is 5%.
     Find the probability that she makes her first false start in her fourth race.
     Give your answer correct to 4 decimal places.
