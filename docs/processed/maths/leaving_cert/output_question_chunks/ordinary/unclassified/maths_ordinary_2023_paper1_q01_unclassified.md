Section A                Concepts and Skills             150 marks

Question 1                                                                       (30 marks)

The prices of houses in a particular area have been increasing over a number of years.
(a)  A house is valued at €240 000 at the start of 2019.
     By the start of 2020, the price of the house has increased by 8%.
     By the start of 2021, the price of the house has increased by a further 9%, based on the
    2020 price.
    Work out the price of the house at the start of 2020, and the price at the start of 2021.





        Price at start of 2020 ൌ ______________     Price at start of 2021 ൌ ______________

(b)   Another house was valued at €460 000.
    One year later, the value of this house had gone up to €472 000.

    Work out the percentage increase in the value of this house over the year.
     Give your answer correct to 1 decimal place.
