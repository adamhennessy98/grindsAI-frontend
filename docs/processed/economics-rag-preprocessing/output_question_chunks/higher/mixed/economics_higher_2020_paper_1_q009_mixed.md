# Marking Scheme

6.   John Maynard Keynes supported the use of fiscal policy by government in an economy.

                 (i)  Define the term fiscal policy:                                               9

      Fiscal policy refers to those actions taken by the government which influence the size,
     timing and magnitude of government spending and taxation.
       Is any change in government spending and taxation that is designed to change overall
     spending in an economy / the use of government spending and taxation to influence
     economic growth.



                  (ii) Do you agree with Keynes support of government intervention in an economy?
              Yes / No.                                                                                  2
               Place a circle around your choice and outline two reasons for your answer.

                                                                                2 x 3
      Yes.

     Achieve sustainable economic growth: the best way for a country to achieve sustainable
     economic growth and avoid large booms followed by prolonged recessions is through
     government intervention in the economy to either dampen down growth in booms or
     encourage growth in recessions.

     Help create employment: intervention by the government helps foster employment
      creation directly by the state or by aiding enterprise through the operation of incentives.

      Redistribution of wealth: government can raise taxation and use this revenue to provide a
      basic incomes to those who may need it.

     Provision of essential services: government can raise taxation and use this revenue to
     provide essential services for citizens of the country.

     Protection of consumers: legislation and regulation may help consumers from exploitation
     by firms / prevent the growth of private monopolies.

     No.

     Anti laissez faire: laissez faire policy dictates that the economy will gravitate towards the
      natural unemployment level based upon the capacity of the economy and the demand for
     and supply of goods and services in the economy. Government intervention is an
     unnecessary approach to economic management.

     Funding by borrowing / increased taxation: government involvement may mean
      additional funding and / or increased taxation which will be borne by the taxpayer.

      Costly / inefficient state services: the services provided may be at higher prices due to
      lack of competition in the market and /or may be inefficient.

     Discourage private enterprise / stifle economic growth: costs for business may increase
     due to increased taxes and /or increased regulation. This could have the effect of reducing
     economic activity within the state.
