# Marking Scheme

8.        Eurozone interest rates were increased four times in the last six months of 2022.
                                                          Adapted from ecb.europa.eu

      (a)   Justify why the European Central Bank (ECB) made this decision.
                                                                                    6
             •  The European Central Bank (ECB) made this monetary policy decision to combat
                 the inflationary / cost of living crisis across Europe.


      (b)  Does being a member of the Eurozone affect Ireland’s ability to implement monetary
            policy in Ireland?  Yes       No                 Explain your answer.


                                                     Yes.

             •   Ireland doesn’t have sovereignty over its monetary policy anymore.
             •   Ireland cannot change its base interest rate - it is set by the ECB.
                                                                                    6             •   Ireland cannot print more currency without permission from the ECB.
             •   Ireland cannot change bank lending regulations independently from ECB
                   control.
