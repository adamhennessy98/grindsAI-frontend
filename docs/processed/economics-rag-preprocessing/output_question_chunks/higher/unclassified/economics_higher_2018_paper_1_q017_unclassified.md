   (i)   Distinguish between a movement along a demand curve and a shift in a demand curve.

     Movement along a demand curve:
    A change in the price of the good itself will cause a movement along the demand
       curve (all other things being equal) and this is referred to as a change in the quantity         5
      demanded.

     A shift in a demand curve:
    A change in any of the factors other than price will cause the demand curve to shift and
        this is referred to as a change in demand, at any given price.                                                                                 5
