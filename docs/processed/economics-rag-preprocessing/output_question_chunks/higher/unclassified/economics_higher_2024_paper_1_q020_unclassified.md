# Marking Scheme

                                           Possible Responses



(a)     (i)            In the Irish economy there are many restaurants in the market. This is an example of     3 @ 5
          monopolistic competition. Outline three characteristics (other than many sellers) of a
21        firm operating in monopolistic competition.


         There are many buyers - an individual customer, by their own actions, cannot
           influence the market price of the food on offer at the restaurant.

         Each firm seeks to maximise profits - the aim of each company is to maximise profits, to
        do this they produce the quantity where MC = MR

        Freedom of entry and exit - no barriers to entry exist in the restaurant industry. It is
          possible for outlets to enter and exit the industry as they wish.

         Reasonable knowledge regarding profits - everyone is assumed to have reasonable
         knowledge as to the profits made by other firms, consumers have reasonable knowledge
           of the prices being charged.

          Product differentiation exists - the foods/ meals on offer from the many restaurants
          are not identical but may be close substitutes. Firms may use advertising and special
           offers to attract consumers i.e. Early Bird Special.


        (ii)  Firms operating under monopolistic competition waste resources.                    1 @ 6
        Do you agree or disagree with this statement? Indicate your choice below by ticking the
           relevant box and justify your choice.

         AGREE:

               It does not produce at the lowest point of average cost, so they are not minimising their
            costs.
           This may be due to spending by the firm on advertising its products or the firm does not
         produce a sufficient quantity to benefit from economies of scale.

         DISAGREE:

                If a firm invests in innovation and increases sales it may not be considered wasteful,
            e.g. Apple’s investment confers an advantage on the company that it would not enjoy
          without such spending.
