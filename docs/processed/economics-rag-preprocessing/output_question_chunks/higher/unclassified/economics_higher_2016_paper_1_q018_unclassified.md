# Question

      (a)    (i)    State a market structure which most closely reflects the situation above, giving a
                reason for your answer.
               (ii)   Outline two other key characteristics of this market structure.
                (iii)  Explain, with the aid of a labelled diagram, the likely shape of the demand curve in this

# Marking Scheme

2.    Define the Law of Diminishing Marginal Returns and illustrate this with a suitable example.
                                                                                                   (16 marks)
      Definition: The Law of Diminishing Marginal Returns states

        if increasing quantities of a variable factor of production are combined with a given quantity of a fixed
      factor of production, a stage will eventually be reached where the addition to total output (the marginal
      returns / output per unit of the variable factor) will begin to decrease.
