# Question

2.      Irish exports increase to record €160bn
        despite the Covid-19 Pandemic.

# Marking Scheme

2.      (a)   Using the data provided, explain, whether the Balance of Payments is likely to                                                                               9
              have
         an overall expansionary or contractionary effect. Refer to relevant figures in the
          diagram above to explain your answer. Indicate by means of a tick ( ) whether it
               is expansionary or contractionary.
          Answer: Expansionary
             •  Exports have increased by 6.4% and imports have increased by 5.4%.
             •  The increase in exports is (1%) larger than the increase in imports thus
                 the injections are larger than leakages which with have an overall
                 expansionary effect on the economy.

                                                                               6      (b)   Outline one factor which could have influenced the above trend in Personal
                                                                                           (3+3)         Consumption in Quarter 2, 2021.

             •  Pent up demand: consumers couldn’t spend during lockdowns.
             •  Increases in income: employees who return to employment following
                lockdowns will now have higher disposable income (than that received on
                PUP, EWSS, TWSS etc).
             •  Increased consumer and business confidence: may encourage consumers
                  to spend on those items which they deferred i.e. holidays.
             •  Expectations of future price increases: with increasing demand
               consumers may fear than prices will increase in the future and so buy now.
