             •  Even if price changes, the venues can only accommodate a certain number of
                  attendees.
             •    It is perfectly inelastic or vertical, reflecting the fact that venues have a fixed
                   capacity.


      (b)    Explain two conditions necessary for price discrimination to occur in the selling of       2 @ 3
              tickets for the Rugby World Cup 2023.

          Consumer ignorance:
          The consumer is unaware that the price discrimination is taking place.
          Consumer indifference:
          Consumers are aware of the price discrimination but don’t care about it.
          Consumer attitudes:
          Consumers feel that it is worth paying more for the product rather than go without it.
          Monopoly power:
          The firm must have some degree of monopoly power in order to price discriminate
           otherwise rival firms would undercut prices and the price discrimination would cease.
             Different price elasticities of demand:
             In order for (third degree) price discrimination to occur the firm must be able to
            recognise that different customers are more or less responsive to price changes and
            therefore price accordingly e.g. pensioners and students would be more price elastic.
          Market separation:
                  It must be impossible for customers to buy the product in the cheaper market and
           then resell it in the more expensive market.
