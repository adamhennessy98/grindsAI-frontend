                 MUx  2500  MUy  1250   MUz   75
                  =    =   =    =    =
                      Px    200    Py    100     Pz     ?


          (a) Calculate the price John would be willing to pay for one unit of good Z. Complete
           your calculations in the box below.

                                2500   1250   75
                           =    =
                                200    100    𝟔                              7


                                    2500 ÷ 200 = 12.5

                                                or

                                    1250 ÷ 100 = 12.5

                                     75 ÷ 12.5 = €6


         (b) Explain why John would pay this price.
