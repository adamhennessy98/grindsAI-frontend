      (b)   Outline three canons/principles of taxation which are achieved through the use of
            direct taxes.                                                                       6

             •   Certainty: it should be clear and unambiguous to the taxpayer the amount they
                 are required to pay and the reason for doing so.
             •  Convenience: the tax such be levied at a time and in a manner which is most
                 convenient for the taxpayer to pay.
             •   Equity: the tax system should operate fairly. An equitable tax system should
                 take a larger amount from those who are more able to pay than others.
             •  Economy: the cost of tax collection should be far exceeded by the amount of tax
                which is collected.
             •  Not act as a disincentive to save/work/invest: no tax should negatively affect
                 those willing to supply their labour / or their ability to save or discourage
                 entrepreneurs from investing.
             •  Evasion should be impossible: otherwise the tax is not effective in raising
                 revenue.
