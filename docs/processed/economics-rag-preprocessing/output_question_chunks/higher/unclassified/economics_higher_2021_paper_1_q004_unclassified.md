     (b)   Outline two reasons why achieving a trade surplus is an objective of the Irish         2 @ 4
         government.

      Increased employment
         If there is an increase in the demand for Irish exports abroad this will lead to an increase
       in demand for Irish labour to produce the goods domestically increasing Irish
     employment.

      Injection into Circular Flow / Economic Growth
       Irish exports represent an injection into the circular flow of income. Injections may also
      create a multiplier effect creating a more than proportional increase in Irish national
     income and generating further employment and improving the standard of living in
       Ireland.

      Imports are a leakage from the Circular Flow of Income.
       Irish imports represent a leakage from the circular flow of income. Leakages may also
      create a negative multiplier effect creating a more than proportional decrease in Irish
      national income and creating foreign employment in place of Irish employment.
