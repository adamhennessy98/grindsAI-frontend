          •  Pent up demand: during the pandemic citizens had built up savings (over €31bn

           (ii)    It is assumed that consumers taking part in economic activity behave rationally.           1st x 6
                                                                                                     2nd x 4             Explain the concept of consumers behaving rationally, using an example to support
            your answer.


           Behaving rationally means the consumer acts in a manner consistent with their
            preferences / consumers buy goods and services with their income that give them
           the maximum satisfaction.


           Example:  If a consumer prefers a pair of trainers to a pair of jeans and they both cost
           the same then the rational consumer will choose the trainers.
                   If the trainers are for sale in two adjoining shops, then the rational consumer will buy
           the trainers at the lower price.
