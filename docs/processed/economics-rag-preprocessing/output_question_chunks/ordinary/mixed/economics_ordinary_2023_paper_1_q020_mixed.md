23        Fixed costs: costs which remain the same regardless of the number of units produced.     6
         They costs don’t vary with output. These costs must be paid even if nothing is

          Examples: rent of premises, insurance, rates, marketing budget, consultancy fees        3


           Variable costs: costs which vary with output i.e. the more that is produced the more it     6

       (ii)    Outline one reason why it is important for a business to continue to earn profit                                                                                   5
              (in the long run).


                 •  Continue production: profits are necessary for the entrepreneur to remain
                        in business.  It is the return on their time and effort to keep the business
                      going.
                 •   Easier to raise finance: the existence of profits will make it easier to raise
                      finance e.g. a bank loan or to get investors to buy shares.
                 •  Fund expansion: profits will enable the business to expand and grow.
                 •  Encourages risk taking: profits are necessary for an entrepreneur to
                    undertake risks
                         •  Reward innovation: a business which earns profit may encourage those
