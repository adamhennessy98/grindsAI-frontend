# Question

1.   Cad a deirtear linn faoi árasán Chantal?               1.   What are we told about Chantal’s
                                                          apartment?

      (a)  Tá sé i gceantar costasach.                           (a)    It’s in an expensive district.

      (b)  Tá sé i bhfad ón ollscoil.                             (b)    It’s far away from the university.

      (c)  Tá sé cóngarach do na siopaí go léir.                  (c)    It’s close to all the shops.

      (d)  Tá sé ar an séú hurlár.                                (d)    It’s on the sixth floor.
