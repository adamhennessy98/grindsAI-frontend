# Question

3.  Cén fáth nach bhfuil Isabelle ag obair            3.  Why doesn’t Isabelle work this year?
        i mbliana?

        Ní féidir léi post a fháil.                       She can’t find a job.

        Tá sí an‐leisciúil.                            She is very lazy.

       Tá go leor airgid aici.                         She has plenty of money.

        Ní ligfidh a hathair di.                        Her father won’t allow her.
