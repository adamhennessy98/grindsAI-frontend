# Question

4.  Sa samhradh, cad a dhéanann Hugo sa           4.   In summer, what does Hugo do in the
     tráthnóna?                                     evening?

    Téann sé amach lena chairde.                 He goes out with his friends.

     Fágann sé na comhlaí lánoscailte.              He leaves the shutters wide open.

     Lasann sé na soilse.                        He turns on the lights.

    Téann sé i gcomhair siúlóid thapa.              He goes for a brisk walk.
