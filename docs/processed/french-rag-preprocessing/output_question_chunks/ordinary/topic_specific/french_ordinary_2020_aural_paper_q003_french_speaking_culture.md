# Question

3. (a)  Cén fhadhb atá ag Agathe?                3. (a)  What problem does Agathe have?



      Ní féidir léi leabhar maith a aimsiú.             She cannot find a good book.

       Caithfidh sí dul a luí go luath.                  She has to go to bed early.

       Níl sí ábalta dul ag siopadóireacht.              She is unable to go shopping.

     Tá claonadh aici titim ina codladh              She tends to fall asleep while reading.
      agus í ag léamh.

    (b)  Cad a deir Agathe faoi leabhar a            (b)  What does Agathe say about
        chríochnú?                                         finishing a book?
