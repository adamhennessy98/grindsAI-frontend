# Question

2.    (i)   Cá fhad ó theach Grégoire atá an baile           2.      (i)  How far is the nearest town from
        mór is cóngaraí?                                         Grégoire’s home?





      (ii)  Cad a rinne Grégoire tar éis argóint                         (ii)   After an argument with his friends,
          lena chairde?                                       what did Grégoire do?

           (a)  Chuaigh sé abhaile ar an mbus.                        (a)  He caught a bus home.

           (b)  Chuaigh sé abhaile i dtacsaí.                           (b)  He got a taxi home.

            (c)   Chuaigh sé abhaile ar rothar.                            (c)  He cycled home.

           (d)   Shiúil sé abhaile.                                      (d)  He walked home.
