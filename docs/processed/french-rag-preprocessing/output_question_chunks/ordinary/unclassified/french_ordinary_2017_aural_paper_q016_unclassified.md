# Question

2.    (i)    Cén fáth ar stop an póilín carr a raibh       2.    (i)   Why did the policeman stop
             beirt fhear istigh ann?                             a car with two men inside?





       (ii)    Cén dochar a rinne an paisinéir                          (ii)   What damage did the passenger
         do charr na bpóilíní?                            do to the police car?

             (a)    Bhris sé scáthán taoibh.                            (a)   He broke the wing mirror.

             (b)     Phoill sé na boinn.                                  (b)   He punctured the tyres.

              (c)    Srac sé an aeróg den charr.                          (c)   He tore off the aerial.

             (d)    Rinne sé smidiríní                                  (d)   He smashed the
               den ghaothscáth.                                   windscreen.
