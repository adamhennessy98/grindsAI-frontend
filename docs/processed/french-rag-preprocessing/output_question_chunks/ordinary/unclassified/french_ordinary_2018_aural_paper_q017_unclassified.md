# Question

3.  Cén pionós atá á ghearradh ar an               3.  What penalty is being imposed on the
      ealaíontóir graifítí?                                        graffiti artist?

         Ní mór dó am a chaitheamh               He must spend time in prison.
          sa phríosún.

         Ní mór dó leithscéal a ghabháil             He must apologise publicly.
        go poiblí.

         Ní mór dó íoc as an nglanadh suas.          He must pay for the clean‐up.


         Ní mór dó a fhearas graifítí                He must hand over his graffiti
         a thabhairt ar láimh.                         equipment.


       CRÍOCH                  END
