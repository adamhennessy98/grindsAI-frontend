            féachaint ar scannáin sa bhaile?                   films at home?



       (b)  Cad a deir Gabrielle faoi dhul go              (b)  What does Gabrielle say about
              dtí an phictiúrlann?                            going to the cinema?

        Tá ticéid an‐chostasach.                             Tickets are very expensive.

        Tá na suíocháin míchompordach.                  Seats are uncomfortable.

        Bíonn na daoine róghlórach.                     People are too noisy.

          Caithfidh tú taisteal chun dul ann.               You have to travel to get there.
