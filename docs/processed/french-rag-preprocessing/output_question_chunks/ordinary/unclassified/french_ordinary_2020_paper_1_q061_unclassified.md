# Question

Question 3

Répondez à (a) ou à (b).




(a)    Tá tú díreach tar éis teach Marie, do            (a)   You have just arrived at your French
       chara Francach, a bhaint amach. Nótáil               friend Marie’s house. Note the
      na rudaí seo a leanas i bhFraincis i do                following in French in your diary:
       dhialann:

          -   Bhí an turas traenach an-fhada                       -   The train journey was very long
          agus an-tuirsiúil.                              and tiring.

          -   Tá máthair Marie an-deas agus                       -    Marie’s mother is very nice,
           labhraíonn sí Béarla.                           and speaks English.

          -    Scríobhfaidh tú chuig do                              -   You will write to your parents
            thuismitheoirí maidin amárach.                  tomorrow morning.



                   OU




(b)    Scríobh litir fhoirmiúil i bhFraincis             (b)    Write a formal letter in French to
       chuig Monsieur le Directeur,                     Monsieur le Directeur, Camping
      Camping des Pins, Passe de Formose,              des Pins, Passe de Formose,
      33780 Soulac-sur-Mer, France.                   33780 Soulac-sur-Mer, France.
      Sa litir:                                                 In the letter:

         -   Abair go bhfuil sé i gceist ag do                       -   Say that your family plans to
           theaghlach dul go dtí an Fhrainc                  go to France in August.
                     i mí Lúnasa.

         -   Abair go bhfuil ceathrar díbh                         -   Say that there are four of you
         ann agus go bhfuil carbhán                     and you have a caravan.
           agaibh.

         -    Iarr air eolas faoin ionad                               -   Ask him to send you
          campála a chur chugat.                            information on the campsite.

         Is tusa Alan Ó Riain/Alice Ní Riain, 8              You are Alan / Alice Ryan,
      Corrán an Úlloird, Biorra, Co. Uíbh                8 Orchard Drive, Birr, Co. Offaly.
        Fhailí.
