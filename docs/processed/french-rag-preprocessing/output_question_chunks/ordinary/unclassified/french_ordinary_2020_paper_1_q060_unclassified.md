Répondez à (a) ou à (b).




 (a)    Fág an teachtaireacht seo a leanas           (a)    Leave the following message in
                i bhFraincis do do chara                         French for your friend
         Paul/Christine, a bhfuil tú ag                        Paul/Christine, with whom you
        fanacht leis/léi in Lille. Abair:                     are staying in Lille. Say that:

            -   Ghlaoigh do chara Sabine ort                         -   Your friend Sabine phoned you
             ar an bhfón ar maidin.                                this morning.

            -   Tá tú ag ól caife léi sa bhaile                           -  You are having coffee with
           mór.                                          her in town.

            -   Ceannóidh tú éisc sa mhargadh                      -  You will buy fish at the
          don dinnéar.                                 market for dinner.
