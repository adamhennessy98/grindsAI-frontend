Répondez à (a) ou à (b).

(a)  Fág an teachtaireacht seo a leanas do              (a)   Leave the following message for your
     do chol ceathrar Thomas / Michelle                    cousin Thomas / Michelle with whom
     a bhfuil tú ag fanacht leis / léi i bPáras.               you are staying in Paris. Say that:
     Abair:

       -   Gur bhuail tú lena c(h)ara Adrien                        -    You met his / her friend Adrien
            tar éis lóin inniu.                                               this afternoon.

       -   Nach bhfuil Adrien ábalta dul go dtí                    -    Adrien cannot go to the disco next
         an dioscó Dé Céadaoin seo chugainn.                  Wednesday.

       -  Go dtiocfaidh sé go dtí an teach                          -   He will come to the house tomorrow
         amárach ag leathuair tar éis a naoi.                           at half past nine.


                    OU





(b)  Tá tú ag obair mar fhreastalaí sa Fhrainc.          (b)  You are working as a waiter / waitress in
     Scríobh cárta poist chuig do chara                     France. Write a postcard to your friend
     Georges / Odile agus abair:                         Georges / Odile in which you say that:

       -  Go bhfuil na custaiméirí go deas, agus                  -    The customers are nice, and the
        go bhfuil an pá go maith.                                  salary is good.

       -  Go bhfuil árasán beag ar cíos agat                       -    You have rented a small flat near
           in aice le lár na cathrach.                                 the city centre.

       -  Go rachaidh tú go Lille Dé Máirt seo                   -    You will go to Lille next Tuesday
         chugainn chun roinnt siopadóireachta                      to do some shopping.
         a dhéanamh.

       Nótáil iad seo a leanas i do dhialann:                Note the following in your diary:

         -   Tá na scrúduithe furasta agus tá tú                     -   The exams are easy and you are very
            an-sásta.                                                     satisfied.

         -   Bhí an Bhitheolaíocht inniu                             -   Biology today was more difficult
