# Question

4.  Cad a dhéanann Christian i rith an lae?         4.  What does Christian do during the day?

     Téann sé chuig traenáil pheile.                    He goes to football training.

     Cuireann sé an t-aerchóiritheoir ar siúl.              He turns on the air conditioning.

    Dúnann sé na fuinneoga agus na comhlaí.            He closes the windows and shutters.

     Léann sé leabhar.                             He reads a book.
