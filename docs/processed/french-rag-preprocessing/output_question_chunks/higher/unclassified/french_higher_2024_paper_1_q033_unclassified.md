6 ................................................................................................................................ 5+5 Marks
In this extract, Dimitri shows a lack of maturity. Do you agree? Refer to the text in support of
your answer. (Two points, about 50 words in total.)
 Candidates may choose to agree and / or disagree using the following points.
 Any two of:
     •  He stays in his bedroom a lot and doesn’t go out much
     •  He wanders aimlessly around the house and the garden
     •  He seems to carry the weight of the world on his shoulders
     •  He denies for some time that there’s anything wrong
     •  He tells his mother about his problems
     •  He says he feels lost and wants to give up his studies
     •  He doesn’t seem to know what to do in the future
     •  He feels he must decide his future because he thinks his friends have done so
     •  He feels he should be able to decide about his future, just like his friends
     •  He has the courage and maturity to stop studying something he does not enjoy
     •  He has the belief in himself to tell his mother what he really wants to do
     •  He has the ambition and determination to go travelling
     •   His ambitions are vague… he wants to see the world
     •  He misinterpreted his mother’s smile accusing her of finding the situation funny
     •  He accepts his mother’s savings in order to travel the world
     •  He goes off travelling on his own
     •  He appreciates what his mother has done for him, hugs her and tells her he loves her
     •  He promises her that she will be proud of him
