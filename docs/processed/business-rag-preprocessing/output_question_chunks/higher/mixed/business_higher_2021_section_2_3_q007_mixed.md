# Question

Question 5                                                          Enterprise/Managing

Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.

                               Ballymakenny Farm Heritage & Speciality Potatoes

                        Maria Flynn and her husband David are potato farmers. Maria saw a
                          niche in the market to start growing their own speciality Purple
                             Violetta Potatoes.
                         Ballymakenny Farm now supply their speciality potatoes to leading
                           chefs and restaurants.





(A)   Discuss the entrepreneurial skills and characteristics you would associate with entrepreneurs
       like Maria and David.                                                                    (20)



(B)   Illustrate the factors that a business needs to consider when communicating with its various
      stakeholders.                                                                            (20)



(C)    (i)   Define the term delegation.

        (ii)   Describe the benefits of delegation for:
                          management
                          the employees.                                                 (20)
