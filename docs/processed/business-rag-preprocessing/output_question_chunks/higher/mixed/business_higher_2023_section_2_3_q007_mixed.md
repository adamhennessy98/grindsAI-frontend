# Question

Question 5                                                          Enterprise/Managing



Jim Gavin, Director of People and Operations in the Irish Aviation Authority,
is a speaker on leadership and motivation. He references motivational
theorists such as Douglas McGregor and Abraham Maslow.





(A)   Outline, giving an example, how a business manager could use each level of Maslow’s
      Hierarchy of Needs to motivate their staff.                                              (25)


(B)   Explain three implications for a manager of adopting a Theory Y approach to management.
                                                                                               (15)


(C)    (i)    Distinguish between enterprise and management.

         (ii)   Discuss two benefits of planning as a management activity.

                                                                                               (20)
