# Question

Question 5                                                               Business in Action

Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.

                     Apple takeover of Beats Electronics

 In 2014, Apple confirmed an acquisition/takeover of headphone maker and music
 streaming service Beats Electronics for a reported fee of $3 billion dollars. As part of
  its business expansion, Apple conducted market research.

(A)    (i)    Explain what is meant by a takeover.

        (ii)   Outline two advantages and one disadvantage of a takeover as a method of
           business expansion.
                                                                                               (20)

(B)   Describe the reasons why a business conducts market research.                         (20)


(C)   Evaluate debt capital versus equity capital as methods of financing expansion for a business.
                                                                                               (20)
