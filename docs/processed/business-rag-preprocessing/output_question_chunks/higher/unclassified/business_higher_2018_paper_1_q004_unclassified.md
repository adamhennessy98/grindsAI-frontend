# Question

3.    Circle the correct option in the case of each of the following statements.

        (i)   An acquisition (takeover), is a form of inorganic growth/ organic growth.

         (ii)   In a merger, businesses trade under their own names/ a new legal entity is created.

         (iii)   In a strategic alliance, businesses share expertise, and the agreement is permanent/
          temporary.

        (iv)  Expansion can be financed by debt capital which includes / does not include
           ordinary share capital.

       (v)   Economies of scale are defined as unit (average) cost / total costs decreasing as
          output increases.
