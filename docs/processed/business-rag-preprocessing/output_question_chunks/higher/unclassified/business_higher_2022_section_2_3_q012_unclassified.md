# Question

Question 7                                                 Managing/Business in Action

Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.


                 The Cleaning Crew
 The Cleaning Crew, a home and industry cleaning company
 based in Wexford, has seen growth in demand for its
 services across the south.  It is considering expanding the
 company across Ireland based on research undertaken.
 Management are considering all finance options to fund
 expansion.


 (A)  The following figures are taken from the final accounts of The Cleaning Crew for 2021 and
     2020.


          The Cleaning Crew              2021                  2020

       Authorised Share Capital             €750,000               €750,000

       Issued Share Capital                 €460,000               €300,000

      Long‐term Loan                    €150,000               €250,000

       Retained Earnings                   €140,000               €120,000


        (i)    Explain the term Debt/Equity Ratio.

        (ii)   Calculate the Debt/Equity Ratio for 2021 and 2020. Show your workings.

         (iii)  Comment as to whether the Debt/Equity Ratio has improved or disimproved?

                                                                                               (25)


 (B)   Apart from the Debt/Equity Ratio, discuss the factors a business should consider when
      choosing a source of finance.                                                            (20)



 (C)   Outline the importance of businesses engaging in market research.                      (15)
