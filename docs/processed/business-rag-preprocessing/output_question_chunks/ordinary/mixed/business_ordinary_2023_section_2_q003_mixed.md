# Question

Question 1                                                           People in Business


Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.


                   Dental implant firm ordered to pay manager €55,000

 A UK dental implants company has been ordered to pay €55,000 to a former Irish sales
 manager who was unfairly dismissed.
                                                                    Adapted from rte.ie


(A)    Outline two reasons for fair dismissal.                                                (15)

(B)    Outline one function of the Workplace Relations Commission.                        (10)





(C)     List five stakeholders in business.                                                     (15)

(D)    Explain the term co-operative relationship and outline one example of a co-operative
       relationship between two stakeholders.                                              (15)

(E)   A legal contract may be terminated by the following methods:
          •   Frustration
          •  Breach
          •  Agreement
          •  Performance

        Explain, using examples, three of these methods.                                     (20)
