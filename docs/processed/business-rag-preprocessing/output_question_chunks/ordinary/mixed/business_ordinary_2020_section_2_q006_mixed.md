# Question

Question 4                                                              Enterprise / Managing



Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.


                                            Vegalicious

 Ciara Brady noticed an increased demand for healthy treats and in particular vegan products.
 She decided to give up her job in marketing and invest her savings into setting up a new business
 called “Vegalicious” which produces a range of vegan treats using natural ingredients.





 Ciara now employs a staff of eight. She pays her staff well and praises them for their efforts. She
 holds weekly meetings with her staff where they discuss new ideas and she consults them on her
 plans for the business. Ciara continuously stresses the importance of good quality control to her
  staff.


(A)   Outline three enterprising characteristics/skills displayed by Ciara.                       (15)


(B)   Outline two rewards for Ciara of setting up her own business.                           (10)


(C)   Outline two advantages of meetings as a method of communication.                    (15)


(D)    (i)   Outline two benefits to Ciara of using a democratic leadership style.

        (ii)    List two other leadership styles.
                                                                                               (20)

(E)   Discuss two reasons why quality control is important for Ciara’s business.                (15)
