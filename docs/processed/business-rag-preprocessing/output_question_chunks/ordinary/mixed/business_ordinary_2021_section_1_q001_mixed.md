Instructions

Write your Examination Number and your Day and Month of Birth in the boxes on the front cover.

Write your answers in blue or black pen. You may use pencil for sketches, graphs and diagrams
only.

There are two sections in this examination. Questions for Section 2 are supplied separately but
your answers must be written in this Answerbook.

Candidates are required to answer:

(A)    Five questions from Section 1

      and

(B)    Three questions from Section 2 as follows:

     One question from Part 1

     One question from Part 2 and

     One other question from either Part 1 or Part 2.

        All questions in this section carry equal marks.


Please note the allocation of marks per question.
