# Question

Question 3                                                           International Environment

Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.


                         Ireland’s Trade in Goods 2019

      Exports                          Imports
      United Kingdom    €16 bn         United Kingdom   €20 bn
      United States      €47 bn         United States     €14 bn
      Rest of World      €90 bn         Rest of World     €55 bn


(A)   Outline two reasons why Ireland exports goods and services.                            (15)

(B)    (i)    Calculate the total visible exports for Ireland in 2019. Show your workings.

        (ii)   Calculate the total visible imports for Ireland in 2019. Show your workings.
                                                                                               (10)

(C)    (i)    Calculate the Balance of Trade for Ireland in 2019. Show your workings.

        (ii)   State whether it is a surplus or deficit.
                                                                                               (15)

(D)   Explain two benefits that Irish consumers receive from international trade.              (15)


(E)    (i)   Outline two challenges for Irish businesses exporting outside the European Union.

        (ii)   Explain how the Irish Government benefits from Irish businesses engaging in
            international trade.
                                                                                               (20)
