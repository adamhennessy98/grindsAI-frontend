# Question

Question 2                                                  Domestic Environment


Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.


 Janet and Michael O’Donnell are considering using a franchise method to
 expand their business. They operate an artisan burger bar, where all food is
 made freshly by Michael every day. The business thrived during the last year
 and they can see potential in franchising the idea.



(A)   Explain the term franchise as a form of business ownership.                             (10)

(B)  Name two other businesses in Ireland which operate as franchises.                      (10)

(C)   Outline one advantage and one disadvantage for Janet and Michael if they decide to
      franchise out their business.                                                             (15)



                    Hospitality industry welcomes the reduction of VAT to 9%

 Budget 2021 saw a decrease in the VAT rate for the hospitality and tourism industries from
 13.5% to 9% until December 2021.                       Adapted from the Irish Examiner




(D)    (i)  Name the sector of the economy in which the hospitality and tourism industries
           operate.

        (ii)   Explain the other two sectors of the economy and give one example of an industry in
          each sector.                                                                       (25)

(E)   Outline one impact the reduction in the VAT rate will have on each of the following:

           Tourism and hospitality industries

           Government Current Income.                                                   (15)
