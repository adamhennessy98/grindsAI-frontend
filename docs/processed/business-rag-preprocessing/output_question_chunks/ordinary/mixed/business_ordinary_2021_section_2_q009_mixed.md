# Question

Question 7                                                             Managing

Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.

                        Eden Hills Golf Club

  Eden Hills Golf Club is a very popular course in the sunny South East of Ireland. It has a
   top‐class course, with a bar that supplies bar food. They want to expand the club house to
   include a function room, to hold bigger events in the future. The golf club has no money saved
  and sees this as a long‐term investment. The Finance Committee is currently producing a
  business plan and a Zoom meeting of all club members has been called.


 Cashflow Forecast Eden Hills Golf Club Ltd for the first 3 months of 2022
         2022               January         February       March           Total
                             €              €             €             €
 Total Receipts (A)                 13,000          14,000        25,000          52,000
 Total Payments (B)               21,000          15,000        13,000          49,000
 Net Cash (A‐B)                     ‐8,000            ‐1,000        12,000            3,000
 Opening Cash                     2,000            ‐6,000         ‐7,000            2,000
 Closing Cash                       ‐6,000            ‐7,000         5,000            5,000


(A)  Name one item Eden Hills Golf Club might include in their cashflow forecast under each of
     the following sections:

        (i)   Receipts section

        (ii)  Payments section.                                                                 (10)

(B)    (i)   Does Eden Hills Golf Club have a better cashflow position in January or March?

        (ii)   Outline two possible reasons for your answer.                                     (20)

(C)   Outline two advantages of meetings as a method of communication.                    (15)

(D)   Explain two sources of long term finance Eden Hills Golf Club could use to expand its
      business.                                                                                (15)

(E)   Outline two benefits of preparing a business plan.                                       (15)
