# Question

Question 5                                                              Enterprise / Managing


Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.





                      SweetKing is an online pick ‘n’ mix store which was founded by Nathan
                   Adams in June 2020. Nathan saw a gap in the market for an online pick
                               ‘n’ mix store with speedy delivery to your door. Nathan sources his
                        stock from Ireland, Belgium, Spain, and the UK. He has since expanded
                           his business into retail.
                                                               Adapted from the examiner.ie




(A)    Outline three enterprising characteristics/skills that entrepreneurs like Nathan display. (15)


(B)      (i)     Explain the term stock control.

           (ii)    Outline two reasons why stock control would be important to a business like
             SweetKing.                                                                     (15)


(C)    Leadership styles include Autocratic, Democratic and Laissez-faire.

       Outline two features of any one leadership style that Nathan could use in his business. (15)


(D)      (i)     Explain the term delegation.

           (ii)    Describe two benefits of delegation for a manager.                             (15)


(E)    Outline two benefits of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) for SweetKing.
                                                                                               (15)
