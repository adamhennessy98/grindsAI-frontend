# Marking Scheme

9.    Explain the following barriers to trade.                        10m

            Tariff: A tax on goods imported into a country from another country.     5m
           Tariffs are charged on goods coming into Ireland from outside the EU.      (3+2)
           Tariffs make the imported goods more expensive and increase tax
         revenue for the Government.

         Quota: A Quota is a limit to the quantity of particular goods coming                                                      5m
          into a country / a limit to the quantity of goods being imported from                                                                                  (3+2)
           specific countries. Quotas are usually designed to protect home
        produced goods, but it may also be a limit on the amount of certain
        goods that can be produced in a country, e.g. milk quota.
