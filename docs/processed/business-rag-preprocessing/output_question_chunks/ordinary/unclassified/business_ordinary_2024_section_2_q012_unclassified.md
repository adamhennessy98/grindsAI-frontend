# Question

Question 7                                                             Managing

Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.

   76% would turn down jobs that do not meet flexible working
                   needs, according to a study.

 Employees are demanding more than just a good wage when seeking
 employment. Research suggests that Human Resource Managers are
 looking at other ways of retaining and attracting staff.


                                               Adapted from rte.ie


(A)   Explain two different types of rewards (financial or non‐financial), other than wages, that
     employers can offer employees.                                                         (15)


(B)   Explain any two of the following functions of a Human Resource Manager:

        (i)   Manpower Planning

        (ii)   Training and Development

         (iii)  Performance Appraisal                                                            (15)


(C)  The Human Resource Manager must prepare a job description and a person specification
     before recruiting new staff.

        (i)    List two items in a job description.

        (ii)    List two items in a person specification.                                            (20)


(D)   Outline two negative impacts that technology has on employees.                        (15)


(E)    Explain the term Employee Empowerment.                                             (10)
