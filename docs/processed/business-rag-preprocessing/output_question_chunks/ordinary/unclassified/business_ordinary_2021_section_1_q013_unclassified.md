# Question

12.   Column 1 is a list of business terms. Column 2 is a list of explanations for these terms.
      One explanation has no match.

            Column 1: Business Terms     Column 2: Explanations
        1    Database              A  The person in the business who decides how
                                             information is collected and used.
        2    Cloud Computing         B  A system of remote servers, hosted by the
                                                   internet, which stores and manages information.
        3    Data Controller          C  A software package which allows the user to
                                             present information using sound, words and
                                             images.
        4    Data Subject           D  An electronic filing system used to store large
                                       amounts of information.
        5    PowerPoint               E  A system which allows business to business
                                         communication without human interaction.
                                        F   The person whose personal information is stored
                                         by a business.

       Match the two lists by placing the letter of the correct explanation under the relevant
       number below.


         Business Term           1          2          3          4          5
