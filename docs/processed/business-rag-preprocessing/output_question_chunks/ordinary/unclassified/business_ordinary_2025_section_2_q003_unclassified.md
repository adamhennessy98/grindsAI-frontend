# Question

Question 1                                                           People in Business


Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.

                        Sale of Goods and Supply of Services Act
                                 1980


(A)   Explain, using examples, two of the following provisions of the Sale of Goods and Supply of
      Services Act 1980:
        (i)   Goods must be of merchantable quality
        (ii)  Goods must be fit for purpose
         (iii)  Goods must be as described
       (iv)  Goods must conform to sample                                                    (15)

(B)   Outline any two forms of redress that consumers are entitled to if any of the provisions of
     the Sale of Goods and Supply of Services Act 1980 are broken.                           (15)

(C)   Describe two advantages to a consumer of taking a case to the Small Claims Court.      (15)
