# Question

Question 3                                                           International Environment


Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.


                  Share of Ireland's Total Exports by Region

   40%

   35%

   30%

   25%

   20%

   15%

   10%

    5%

    0%
             Rest of World    Northern Ireland  European Union    Great Britian      United States


(A)      (i)     Using the information above, identify the region to which Ireland exports the most.

           (ii)     List three examples of goods/services that Ireland exports.                    (15)


(B)   Name two barriers to trade and explain one of them.                                 (15)


(C)     Ireland’s Balance of Payments was €15.8 billion from October – December 2022.

          (i)     Explain the term Balance of Payments.

           (ii)    Outline one impact of an increase in exports on the Balance of Payments.     (15)





           In 2023 Ireland marks the 50th anniversary since joining the European Union.



(D)      (i)     Outline two benefits to Ireland of being a member of the European Union.

           (ii)    Describe one challenge of European Union membership to Ireland.            (20)


(E)   Name two institutions of the European Union.                                        (10)
