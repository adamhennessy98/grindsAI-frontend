# Question

Question 8                                                               Business in Action





(A)  Many of the products sold at Supervalu are mass‐produced.

        (i)    Explain the term mass production. Use an example to illustrate your answer.

        (ii)  Name one other production method.                                              (15)


(B)   Supervalu is part of the Musgrave Group, which is an Irish food wholesaler.

        (i)    Explain the term wholesaler.

        (ii)    Illustrate, using a diagram, a channel of distribution that includes a wholesaler.    (20)


(C)   SuperValu stocks a range of own‐brand products.
      Explain, including an example, the term own‐brand products.                           (10)


(D)   Outline two sales promotion techniques that retailers such as SuperValu could use to
      increase sales.                                                                           (15)


(E)   Outline two benefits for SuperValu of sponsoring the All‐Ireland Football Championship. (15)
