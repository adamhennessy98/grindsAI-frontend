# Question

Question 6                                                           Business in Action

Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.

                    Dunmackey House Hotel

 Dunmackey House Hotel has struggled to remain open over the past year. Aideen McNally,
  the Human Resource Manager, had to make seven staff redundant during this time. Aideen
  did not like this aspect of manpower planning and hopes they will be able to re‐hire staff in the
  future. All staff were paid redundancy based on six weeks’ pay per year of service.


(A)   Explain three methods, other than wages, of rewarding employees.                      (15)


(B)   Outline two functions, other than manpower planning, which are part of a Human Resource
     Manager’s job.                                                                          (20)





 Tom Delaney earned €490 per week in the Hotel and worked there for 11 years. He decided to
  use his redundancy package to purchase a van and provide a delivery service for the local
  retailers in Dunmackey who were relying on online/phone in sales for survival. He needed to
  purchase motor insurance for business use on the van and was hoping his premium would not
  be too high.



 (C)  Calculate Tom’s redundancy package from Dunmackey House Hotel.                     (10)

 (D)   (i)  Name and explain one type of motor insurance Tom could purchase.

        (ii)  Name one factor which might impact on a motor insurance premium.             (15)

 (E)  Tom must adhere to the principles of Utmost Good Faith, Indemnity and Insurable Interest.
      Explain two of these principles.                                                          (15)
