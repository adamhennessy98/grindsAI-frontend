# Question

Question 8                                                               Business in Action

Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.



                                    Cadbury has launched its latest products.

                                  The stages of the new product development process
                                        included Idea Generation, Product Screening and
                                               Feasibility Study. Cadbury also engaged in test
                                       marketing.





(A)   Explain two of the underlined terms above.                                             (15)


(B)   Outline two methods of field research that Cadbury may have used as part of its test
     marketing stage.                                                                        (15)


  Cadbury bars are manufactured using mass production. The bars are wrapped using packaging
  with the distinctive purple Cadbury colour.





(C)    (i)    Explain the term mass production.

         (ii)  Name and explain two other methods of production.                              (20)


(D)   Illustrate, using a diagram, the channel of distribution Cadbury might use to
      distribute their chocolate bars.                                                           (10)



(E)   Outline two functions of product packaging.                                             (15)
