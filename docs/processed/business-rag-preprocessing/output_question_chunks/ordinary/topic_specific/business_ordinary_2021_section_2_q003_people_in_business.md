# Question

Question 1                                                       People in Business

Read the information supplied and answer the questions which follow.

               CONSUMER LAW
 Consumers in Ireland are protected by a number of
 different laws. The CCPC is responsible for ensuring all
 consumer laws’ are implemented by businesses and they
 protect consumers rights. The Sale of Goods and Supply of
 Services Act, 1980, outlines consumers rights and retailers’
 responsibilities in relation to goods and services.


(A)    (i)   What do the letters CCPC stand for?
        (ii)  Name the other consumer law which the CCPC enforces.                           (10)

(B)   Explain, using examples, the following provisions of the Sale of Goods and Supply of Services
      Act, 1980:
        (i)   Goods must be of merchantable quality
        (ii)  Goods must be fit for purpose
         (iii)    If a consumer purchases a faulty product who is responsible?                      (20)

(C)   Outline two forms of remedy/redress that consumers are entitled to if any provisions of the
      Sale of Goods and Supply of Services Act, 1980 are broken.                              (15)



   ‘Unofficial strike causes severe disruption to bus services in Limerick’

(D)   Describe any two functions of a trade union.                                            (15)

(E)   Explain the process employees must engage in to hold an ‘official strike’, as set out in the
      Industrial Relations Act, 1990.                                                           (15)
