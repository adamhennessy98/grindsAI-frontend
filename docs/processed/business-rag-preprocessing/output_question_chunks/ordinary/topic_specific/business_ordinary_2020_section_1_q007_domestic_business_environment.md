# Question

        (i)    Inflation refers to an increase / a decrease in the general level of prices from one year
           to the next.


        (ii)  A rise in the employment rate results in an increase / a decrease in income tax
           received by the government.


         (iii)  Lower interest rates mean the cost of borrowing is cheaper / more expensive.


       (iv)    If the euro (€) increases in value relative to the US Dollar ($), Irish products become
