# Question

6.   Circle the correct option in each of the following statements.

      (i)  When there is clear, complete and unconditional acceptance of an offer, there is

       an invitation to treat / a legal agreement.

       (ii)  In a contract, each party must give something of value to the other party and this refers

        to consideration / consent to contract.

       (iii) Some contracts must be in writing if they are to be legally valid. This refers to

         legality of form / legality of purpose.

      (iv) Persons under the influence of alcohol or drugs do / do not have the capacity to enter

       a legal contract.
