             issue. In this case Goggin has an authorised share capital of €900,000 €1

         (ii)   Trade Creditors: Goods are bought on credit from creditors and
           paid for at a later date. In this case they are €53,000.                          [10]

          (iii)   Liquid Assets: These are current assets that can be turned into cash quickly.
          They are current assets less closing stock. In this question they are
          €94,000 ‐ €16,000 = €78,000.                                                  [10]

        (iv)   Interest Paid: This is the extra money paid to the lender for the use of money
          borrowed from a bank.  It is the cost of borrowing money. Goggin Ltd
          had to pay €8,000 interest on the money he borrowed.                        [10]
