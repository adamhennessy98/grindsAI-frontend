(b)    (i)    Depreciation:
            This is the loss in value of a fixed asset during the year due to wear and tear or
          the passage of time. A business will decide a suitable percentage for the yearly
           charge. €50,000 depreciation in the above balance sheet.

         (iii)   Intangible Assets: These are fixed assets that have a value but you cannot see
           or touch. Examples are goodwill and patents.

       (iv)  7% Debenture 2023/2024: Debentures are long term loans. They will be repaid
             in full during the years 2023/2024. They carry a fixed annual rate of interest of

(c)   No, Kennedy Ltd would not have difficulty as the acid test ratio is 1.76:1 which is
