(b)    (i)  9% Debenture 2021/2022                                                [40]
          Debentures are long term loans. They will be repaid in full during the years
          2021/2022. They carry a fixed annual rate of interest of 9%. Springfield Ltd has
           a Debenture loan of €80,000                                              [10]

        (ii)  Trade Debtors: Goods are sold on credit to Debtors and payment is received for
           these goods at a later date. Springfield Ltd has trade creditors of €32,000   [10]

        (iii)  Depreciation: This is the loss in value of a Fixed Asset during the year due to
          wear and tear or the passage of time. A business will decide a suitable % for the
           yearly charge. There is depreciation of €30,000 in the above Balance Sheet. [10]

       (iv)  Interest Paid: This is the extra money paid to the lender for the use of money
          borrowed from a bank.  It is the cost of borrowing money. Springfield Ltd paid
          €9,000 interest at a rate of 9%                                             [10]
