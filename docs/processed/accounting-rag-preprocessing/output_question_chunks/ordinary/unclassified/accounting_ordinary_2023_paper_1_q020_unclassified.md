# Question

8.     Absorption Costing

      Fahy Ltd, a manufacturing company has two departments, production and servicing.

      The budgeted overheads for the two departments for the coming year are as follows:


                                       €                   €

             Department                 Fixed                 Variable

              Production                45,000                18,000

                Servicing                  11,000                16,000


      Fahy Ltd estimates (budgets) direct labour hours for each department for the coming year
       as follows:


             Department                             Direct Labour Hours

              Production                               9,000 hours

                Servicing                                 5,000 hours


        (a) Calculate the overhead absorption rate (both fixed and variable) for each department
         based on direct labour hours.

        (b) The details of customer’s Job no. 1977 are as follows:


                Direct materials                           €2,750

                Direct labour                              €1,275

              Hours in production                     8 hours

              Hours in servicing                       5 hours


           Calculate the total cost of Job no. 1977


         (c) Calculate the selling price of Job no. 1977 if the mark up on cost is 20%.

        (d) State two reasons why a business needs to calculate the cost price of a product.


                                                                                     (80 marks)
