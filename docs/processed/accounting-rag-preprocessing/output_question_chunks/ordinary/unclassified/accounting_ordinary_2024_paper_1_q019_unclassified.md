# Question

8.   Marginal Costing

     Jones Ltd manufactures a single product. The following is the proposed annual budget for
     the coming year:



                                           €              €

                     Sales (40,000 units)                          600,000
                     Variable costs              320,000
                    Fixed costs                 143,185          (463,185)
                  Net profit                                   136,815


     Required:

      (a)   Calculate the selling price per unit.

      (b)   Calculate the variable cost per unit.

       (c)   Calculate the contribution per unit sold.

      (d)   Calculate the break‐even point in volume (units) and sales value (€).

      (e)   Calculate the margin of safety in units and sales value, if the budgeted
            sales for the period are 35,000 units.

        (f)   Prepare a marginal costing statement which includes the following:



                      -   Increase the selling price by €1.50

                      -  Reduce sales volume by 10%

                      -   All other costs remain the same



       (g)   Explain the term ‘Fixed Cost’ in relation to Jones Ltd.
          Give one example of a ‘fixed cost’ that Jones Ltd might incur.
                                                                                (80 marks)
