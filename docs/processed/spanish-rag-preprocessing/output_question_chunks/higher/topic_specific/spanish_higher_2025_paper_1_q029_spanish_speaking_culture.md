# Question

1.   (b) You read an article in a Spanish newspaper about over-tourism in Spain.
       You decide to write a LETTER / EMAIL in Spanish to the editor of the newspaper.

        (You may loosely base your letter/email on the points mentioned below, either agreeing or
        disagreeing with them. You should make five relevant points out of the eight below and
       each of these points should be expanded and developed.)

        •   Many Spanish people feel tourism is out of control.
        •    Foreign visitors create a lot of employment.
        •      Irish people love the good weather in Spain.
        •    Spanish festivals are famous all over the world.
        •    Food is cheaper in Spain than in Ireland.
        •   Some foreign visitors create problems in Spain.
        •    People going on holidays to Spain should learn basic Spanish.
        •     Local people should have access to affordable housing.
