# Question

Q 2. Answer either (a) DIARY ENTRY or (b) NOTE.                                  (20 marks)

      (a)  You are attending the Benicàssim Music Festival with some friends.

          Write a diary entry in Spanish mentioning any four of the following points:

           −  Say that the festival is amazing. Explain why.

           −  Mention what you like about the area where you are staying.

           −  Say how you met one of your new Spanish friends.

           −  Say you want to buy a t-shirt as a souvenir.

           −  Mention what you are going to do after the festival.

                               OR


      (b)  You are staying at a student residence at the University of Malaga.

          Leave a note in Spanish for the receptionist, Clara. Include four of the following points:

           −  Thank her for the book about Andalusia that she gave you.

           −  Say you have gone to the city to visit the Picasso Museum.

           −  Say that you did not sleep well because of the heat.

           −  Ask her if you could move to a different room.

           −   Tell her that classes start tomorrow, you are going to bed early.
