# Question

3.  Busca en el texto una palabra o frase que tenga el mismo sentido (más o menos) que
     las siguientes:                                                                     (5 marks)

(a)  famosa (1)

(b) orígenes (2)

(c)  alternativas (4)


(d) competiciones (6)


(e)  socios (8)

# Marking Scheme

Q. 3   4 + 3 + 3 = 10 marks
        [First correct answer=4m]
       Three of:

                  -    It didn’t let him die of hunger or thirst during the 10 days
                  -  He felt the same as before
                  -  He hasn’t changed inside or outside
                  -    It is people who have changed
                  -   His friends are more his friends than before
                  -   His enemies are more his enemies
                  -  When people see him (on the street) they look at him as if he is a rare/strange
                animal
                  -  He didn’t try/make any effort to be a hero                       (4+3+3m)
