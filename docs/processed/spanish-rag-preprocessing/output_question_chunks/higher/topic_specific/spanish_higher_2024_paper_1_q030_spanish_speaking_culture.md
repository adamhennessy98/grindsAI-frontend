# Question

Q 2. Answer either (a) DIARY ENTRY or (b) NOTE                                   (20 marks)

      (a)  You have spent the year studying in Salamanca, you are returning home.

          Write a diary entry in Spanish mentioning any four of the following points:

           −  Say that the experience was amazing. Explain why.

           −  Mention two things you like about living in Spain.

           −  Describe a day trip you took with your brother last month.

           −  Mention how you plan to keep improving your Spanish.

           −  Say how you are going to spend your last weekend in Spain.

                             OR

      (b)  You are working in a restaurant in Mijas, you are about to take a break.

          Leave a note in Spanish for the manager, Elena. Include four of the following points:

           −  Say that the chef just rang to say that he is sick.

           −   Tell her that you could help in the kitchen.

           −  You have cleaned the tables and the bathroom.

           −  Mention that you are really happy today. Explain why.

           −  Ask for a day off next week to visit your cousin in Estepona.
