# Question

Question 1. Answer EITHER (a) DIALOGUE CONSTRUCTION or (b) LETTER WRITING    (30 marks)

Question 1 (a)  You have just arrived in Murcia, where you meet your new Spanish flat-mate,
                 Gabriela. Complete in Spanish the following dialogue.
              You should attempt any five of the following six points:

Gabriela:    ¡Bienvenido/a a Murcia! ¿Qué tal el viaje?

Tú:        Say that it was a long journey because you had to get up very early to get a bus to
            Dublin airport. Then your flight was delayed because of the bad weather.





Gabriela:    Ay, ¡qué mala suerte! ¿Dónde están tus maletas?

Tú:           Tell her that you only have your rucksack, as your suitcase didn’t arrive from Dublin.
          The airline is looking for it and they are going to call you when they find it.





Gabriela:   A mí me ha pasado varias veces. Las aerolíneas son un desastre.

Tú:        Say that she is right, the worst thing is that you have no summer clothes and you
          need to go shopping. Ask if there is a shopping centre nearby.
