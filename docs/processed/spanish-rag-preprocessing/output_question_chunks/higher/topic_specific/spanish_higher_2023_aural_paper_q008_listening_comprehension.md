# Question

Question 7

UNA NOTICIA:   This will be played three times: first right through, then in two segments with a
                long pause after each segment, and finally, right through again.

                                  EL SECRETO DEL AMOR
                                 (THE SECRET OF LOVE)

(a)  What is said about Julio and Meena’s combined age?





(b)  Where exactly did Julio live?





                                     * PAUSE

(c)  Why were they awarded the Guinness World Record? Give full details.





(d)  What do they say is the secret of love? Give full details.





                   END OF LISTENING COMPREHENSION TEST

APPENDIX ONE
             REASONABLE ACCOMODATIONS & MODIFIED MARKING SCHEME
A modified marking scheme is applied to the work submitted by candidates who have been granted
one or a combination of the following arrangements:

•  A recorded version of written work.
•  Use of a computer with the spell-check facility enabled.
•  Use of a scribe.
•  Waiver in relation to spelling and grammar.


For those candidates who are granted a spelling and grammar waiver, errors in certain grammatical
areas are not penalised. In assessing the work of these candidates, a modified marking scheme will
apply.

Aural
As this part of the examination is answered in English there is no special consideration given, as
incorrect spelling and grammar are not penalised in the standard marking scheme.

Sections A and B
For questions that are answered in Spanish, incorrect spelling and certain elements of grammar such
as verb endings and agreement of adjectives will not be penalised.
