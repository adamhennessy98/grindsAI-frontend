# Question

Q 1. (b) A newspaper article states that Spanish people should spend time in Ireland to
        improve their English.
        You decide to write a letter/email in Spanish to the editor of the newspaper.

     (You may loosely base your letter/email on the points mentioned below, either agreeing or
      disagreeing with them. You should make five relevant points out of the eight below and each
      of these points should be expanded and developed)

               Spanish people don’t need a visa to live in Ireland.
               There are excellent language schools all over the country.
               Spanish people can work part‐time to pay for their studies.
               Irish people are very friendly and the accent is easy to understand.
               There are cheap flights between Spanish and Irish airports.
              The cost of living is expensive.
              The scenery is amazing, especially along the coasts.
               Ireland is great for people who like music and culture.
