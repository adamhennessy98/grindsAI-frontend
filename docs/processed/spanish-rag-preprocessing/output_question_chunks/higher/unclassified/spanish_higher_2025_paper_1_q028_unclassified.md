# Question

Question 1. Answer EITHER (a) DIALOGUE CONSTRUCTION or (b) LETTER WRITING     (30 marks)

Question 1 (a)  You are talking to your Spanish friend Jorge about his plans to study abroad next
                 year. Complete in Spanish the following dialogue.
              You should attempt any five of the following points:

Jorge:     He conseguido una beca Erasmus para pasar un curso académico en una universidad
             extranjera el año que viene. ¡Qué ilusión!

Tú:           Tell him that’s great news, you are delighted because the process to go on Erasmus
                is very long and difficult, especially the personal essay and the interview.





Jorge:      Pues sí, estoy muy contento, y ¡mi familia está muy orgullosa!

Tú:        Say that your sister did Erasmus in Austria three years ago, she enjoyed the
            experience and made friends from different countries. Ask him where he’s planning
            to study.





Jorge:       Pues, no sé. La beca me permite elegir cualquier país de la Comunidad Europea.

Tú:        You would love to go to Spain because it has such a rich culture. You’ve heard that
           Salamanca is one of the oldest universities in the world.
