# Question

Question 2 (a) Diary Entry OR
Question 2 (b) Note

Question 1 (a) You have just arrived at the youth hostel. Complete in Spanish the dialogue.
            You should answer any five of the following points:

Raquel:    Buenos días, ¿en qué puedo ayudarte?

Tú:         Introduce yourself and say that you have just arrived in San Sebastián. Tell her that
          you booked a room for three nights on the hostel’s website.





Raquel:     Perfecto, ya la veo. ¿Qué vas a hacer aquí?
Tú:         Explain that you are going to spend two months here. You are doing a Spanish
           language course in the university.





Raquel:      Y, ¿dónde vas a alojarte después de aquí?

Tú:        Say that you want to share an apartment with some Spanish students to improve
           your Spanish. You are here alone and you don’t know anybody.

(b)  You read an article saying that young people are addicted to their mobile phones.

               Mobile phones can be beneficial for language students.

               Mobile phones are part of life nowadays.
