# Question

Question 1. Answer EITHER (a) DIALOGUE CONSTRUCTION or (b) LETTER WRITING    (30 marks)

Q 1 (a)  You are spending a year in Madrid and you want to join a local basketball club.
        Complete in Spanish the dialogue.
       You should attempt any five of the following points:

Entrenador: Buenos días, ¿así que quieres jugar al baloncesto con nosotros?
Tú:        Say that you are delighted to meet him. Say that you are going to spend a year
            studying in Madrid and that you would like to stay fit.





Entrenador: Estupendo. ¿Juegas bien al baloncesto?
Tú:        Say that you have never played basketball, you prefer Gaelic football. There was a
          good team in your school and they won the final last year.





Entrenador: Si, conozco el fútbol gaélico. ¡Los mejores jugadores deben ser muy ricos!
Tú:        Say that they don’t earn anything, they love the sport. The GAA is a voluntary
            organisation and a very important part of Irish culture.
