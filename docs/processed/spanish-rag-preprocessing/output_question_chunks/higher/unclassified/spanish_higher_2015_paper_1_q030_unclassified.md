# Question

2.    Answer either (a) DIARY ENTRY or (b) NOTE.                                (20 marks)

(a)   You have just spent your birthday in Granada with your Spanish friend Carmen and her
       family.

       Write a DIARY ENTRY in SPANISH mentioning all of the following points:

        −  Say that you had a very nice time.

        −  Mention one thing that you and Carmen’s family did that evening.

        − How you feel about celebrating your birthday in Spain.

        −  Say how you celebrate your birthday in Ireland.

                           OR

(b)   Your Spanish friend is staying with you for a month. It’s the football final today.

      Leave a NOTE in SPANISH for your friend including all of the following points:

        −  Say that you have gone to the football final in Aughrim.

        −  Tell him that Pat Murphy will pick him up around two o’clock.

        −  Remind him to wear the yellow team jersey.

        −  After the match, there will be soup and sandwiches in the sports complex.
