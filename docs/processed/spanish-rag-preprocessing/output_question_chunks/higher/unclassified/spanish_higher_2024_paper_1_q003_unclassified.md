# Question

Section C       Written Production                                      50 marks


Answer all Sections, A, B and C.

Write your Examination Number and your Day and Month of Birth in the boxes on the front cover.

Write your answers in blue or black pen. Do not use pencil.

Write your answers in the spaces provided in this booklet. There is space for extra work at the
end of the booklet. Label any such extra work clearly with the question number and part.

This examination booklet will be scanned and your work will be presented to an examiner on
screen. Anything that you write outside of the answer areas may not be seen by the examiner.





Leaving Certificate Examination 2024              3

# Marking Scheme

SECTION C                                        TOTAL MARKS: 50

Candidates are required to attempt either Q1 (a) or 1 (b) AND either Q2 (a) or 2 (b)

Guidelines for Section C
   •  Should a candidate answer parts (a) and (b) of Question 1 or Question 2, both must be
      marked and marks will be awarded only for the higher scoring of the two.
   •  Minor inaccuracies (i.e. which do not distort ‘message’) should be ignored.
   •  Verbs must be correct for full marks.

N.B.:  In any part of written production the same mistake will not be penalised twice.
