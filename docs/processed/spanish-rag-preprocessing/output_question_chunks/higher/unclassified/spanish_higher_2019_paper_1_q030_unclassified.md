# Question

2.   Answer either (a) DIARY ENTRY or (b) NOTE.                               (20 marks)

      (a)  You are in Almeria. Your flight home has been cancelled.

          Write a DIARY ENTRY in SPANISH mentioning all of the following points:

             Say that your parents are very angry, explain why.

             You feel great because it’s the best hotel you have stayed in.

             There is a fabulous pool, free Wi‐Fi and you can be online all day (and night).

             Say what your plans are for the remainder of your stay.

                                OR

      (b)  You have arranged to go to the cinema with your Spanish friend Pilar.

          Leave a NOTE in SPANISH inviting Fernando to come along.
           Include all of the following points:

             Say that your friend Pilar has asked you to go to the cinema.

             Ask Fernando if he would like to come too.

             You have never seen a film in Spanish before.

             You hope that it will be easy to understand.
