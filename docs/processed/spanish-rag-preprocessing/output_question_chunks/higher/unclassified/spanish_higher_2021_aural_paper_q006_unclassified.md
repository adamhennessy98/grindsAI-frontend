# Question

Question 6

EL TIEMPO:      This will be played three times right through with a long pause after the SECOND
                 hearing.


(a)  What will the weather be like in the North West? Give full details.





(b)  What weather is expected at the weekend? Give full details.





(c)  What will the weather be like in the Canary Islands? Give full details.
