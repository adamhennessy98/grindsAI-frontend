# Question

3.    Busca en el texto una palabra o frase que tenga el mismo sentido (más o menos) que las
       siguientes:                                                                          (5 marks)

        (a)  las doce (1) ________________________________________________________________

        (b)  distinta (2) _________________________________________________________________

        (c)  tiendas (3) _________________________________________________________________

        (d)  introducir (4) ______________________________________________________________

        (e)  tradición (6) _______________________________________________________________

# Marking Scheme

3.     Diálogo – HABLANDO CON ANTONIO BANDERAS, ACTOR FAMOSO

       (2+1) + (1+1) + (1+1+1) + 3 marks                 = 11 marks

a.       (i) It was under a (military) dictatorship (ii) many actors had problems with the police. (2+1 marks)

b.     Because she had children and it was more convenient for the family.                 (1+1 marks)

c.    He hates fast food, he misses Spanish food especially paella / shell fish/ seafood.    (1+1+1 marks)

d.    Three of: Spend a lot of time on the street, chatting, going for a walk, dining/ eating.     (3 marks)
