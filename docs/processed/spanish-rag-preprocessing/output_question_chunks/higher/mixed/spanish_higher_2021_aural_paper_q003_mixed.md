# Question

Question 3

DIÁLOGO: This will be played three times: first right through, then in two segments with a long
          pause after each segment, and finally, right through again.

                HABLANDO CON LA ACTRIZ ESPAÑOLA, ALBA FLORES
                   (SPEAKING WITH THE SPANISH ACTRESS, ALBA FLORES)

(a)  What does she say about her grandparents and flamenco? Give full details.





(b)  How did people treat her before 2017? Give full details.





                                      * PAUSE


(c)  Why is there a lot of work for her in Spain now?





(d)  What advice would she give to young people? Give full details.





Leaving Certificate Examination 2021              4
