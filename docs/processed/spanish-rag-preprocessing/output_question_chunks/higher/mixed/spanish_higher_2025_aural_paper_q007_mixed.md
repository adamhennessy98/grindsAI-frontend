# Question

Question 6

EL TIEMPO:      This will be played three times right through with a long pause after the SECOND
                 hearing.

(b)  What will the weather be like in Andalusia and Murcia? Give full details.





(c)  What is said about the temperatures in the Canary Islands? Give full details.
