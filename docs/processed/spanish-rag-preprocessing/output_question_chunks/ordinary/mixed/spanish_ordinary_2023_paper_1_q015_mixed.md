# Question

Question 2                                                                       (20 marks)

Answer (a) or (b).

      (a)  You are staying near Malaga with your Spanish friend, Pedro.
          Leave him a note, in Spanish, which includes any four of the following points:

               ‐   Say that you are going to the shopping centre.

               ‐    Explain what you are going to buy there.

               ‐   Ask him if he would like to go to the cinema this evening.

               ‐    Tell him you will be in the city centre at 8pm.

               ‐   Ask what his plans are for tomorrow.

                                 OR

      (b)  You are doing a language course in Valencia.
          Write a diary entry, in Spanish, including any four of the following points:

               ‐   Say who is in Valencia with you.

               ‐   Mention how you travelled to Valencia.

                ‐   Say you are learning a lot of Spanish.

               ‐   Describe the food.

               ‐   Mention one place you are going to visit.
