# Question

Question 2                                                                       (20 marks)

Answer (a) or (b).

      (a)  You are staying in Granada with a Spanish friend, José.
          Leave him a note, in Spanish, including any four of the following points:

               -   Say you are going to the market.

               -    Tell him what you will buy there.

               -   Ask if he would like to go out to dinner this evening.

               -    Tell him that you will be in the main square at 9pm.

               -   Say you will ring him later.

                                 OR

      (b)  You are on a school tour in Barcelona.
          Write a diary entry, in Spanish, including any four of the following points:

               -   Describe where you are staying.

               -   Give your opinion on the food.

               -   Describe the weather.

               -   Mention two things you have done so far.

               -   Say you would like to go to the football stadium to see a match.
