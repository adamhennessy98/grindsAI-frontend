# Question

2.   Attempt either (a) or (b).                                                    (20 marks)

      (a)  You are staying in Santander with your Spanish friend, Begoña.
          Leave her a note, in Spanish, that includes all of the following details:

               -    Tell her you are going to the doctor this morning.

               -    Explain what is wrong with you.

               -   Say what time you will return.

               -   Ask if she would like to watch a film on TV later.

                                 OR

      (b)  You are spending some time with a Spanish friend, Pedro, in Alicante.
          Write a diary entry, in Spanish, including all of the following details:

               -   Say you love Alicante because the weather is nice.

               -   Describe where Pedro lives.

               -   Mention what you did yesterday.

               -   Say you are going to a party on Saturday with Pedro and his friends.
