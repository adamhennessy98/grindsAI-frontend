# Question

Question 1                                Letter / E‐mail                           (40 marks)

     Your friend Pablo has invited you to visit him in Santander after the exams.
     Write a letter /e‐mail to him in Spanish.

     Use any five of the points mentioned below:

        ‐    Tell him you are very tired because you are studying for your exams.
        ‐   Say what you did to celebrate your birthday last weekend.
        ‐   Mention two things you like about school.
        ‐    Tell him about your part‐time job.
        ‐   Describe how you will get to Santander.
        ‐   Mention what you would like to do in Santander.
        ‐   Give him your opinion on Spanish food.
        ‐    Tell him about your plans for the rest of the summer.
