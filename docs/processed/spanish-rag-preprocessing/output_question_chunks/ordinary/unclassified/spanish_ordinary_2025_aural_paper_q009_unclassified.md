APPENDIX ONE

               REASONABLE ACCOMODATIONS & MODIFIED MARKING SCHEME

A modified marking scheme is applied to the work submitted by candidates who have been granted one
or a combination of the following arrangements:

  A recorded version of written work.
  Use of a computer with the spell‐check facility enabled.
  Use of a scribe.
  Waiver in relation to spelling and grammar.



For those candidates who are granted a spelling and grammar waiver, errors in certain grammatical
areas are not penalised. In assessing the work of these candidates, a modified marking scheme will
apply.

Aural
As this part of the examination is answered in English there is no special consideration given, as
incorrect spelling and grammar are not penalised in the standard marking scheme.
