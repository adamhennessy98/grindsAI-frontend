# Marking Scheme

Questions must be answered in the language indicated on the examination paper.

In Section A where a candidate answers in the incorrect language (i.e. Spanish instead of English/or
vice versa), the following penalties will apply:
• If the entire question is answered in the incorrect language, mark as per marking scheme and award
50% of the marks achieved.
• Where one or more sub‐parts of a question, but fewer than all are answered in the incorrect
language award 0m for the ones answered in the wrong language.
• If questions are answered in both languages, marks should be awarded for the correct language as
per the marking scheme and ignore the incorrect language.
