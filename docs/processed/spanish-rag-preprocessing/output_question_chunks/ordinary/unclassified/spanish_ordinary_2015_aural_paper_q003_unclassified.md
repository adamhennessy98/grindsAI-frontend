# Question

                 long pause after each segment, and finally, right through again.

           HABLANDO CON ANTONIO BANDERAS, ACTOR FAMOSO
              (TALKING TO ANTONIO BANDERAS, A FAMOUS ACTOR)

     (a)   Where did Antonio start working?

         __________________________________________________________________________


     (b)   When did he move to Hollywood?

          __________________________________________________________________________

                                        * PAUSE

     (c)   According to Antonio, what do Spanish people like to do? Give one detail.

        ___________________________________________________________________________
