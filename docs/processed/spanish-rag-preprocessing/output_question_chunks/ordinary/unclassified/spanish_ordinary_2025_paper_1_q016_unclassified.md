# Question

Question 2                                                                       (20 marks)

Answer (a) or (b).

      (a)  You are staying in Palma de Mallorca with your Spanish friend, Sara.
          Leave her a note, in Spanish, including any four of the following points:

               ‐   Say you are going to the city centre by bus.

               ‐    Tell her you are going shopping.

               ‐   Ask if she would like to go to the cinema with you at 8pm.

               ‐   Say you will go for tapas later.

               ‐    Tell her you will not be home for dinner.

                                 OR

      (b)  You are doing a language course in Salamanca.
          Write a diary entry, in Spanish, including any four of the following points:

               ‐   Say you are doing a language course for two weeks.

               ‐   Describe your teacher.

               ‐   Say that you get a lot of homework.

               ‐   Say you are speaking Spanish every day.

               ‐   Describe the weather in Salamanca.
