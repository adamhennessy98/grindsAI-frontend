# Question

Question 2                                                                       (20 marks)

Answer (a) or (b).

      (a)  You are staying in Vigo, with a Spanish friend, Luis.
          Leave him a note, in Spanish, including any four of the following details:

               ‐   Say there is a football match this afternoon.

               ‐    Tell him you are going to the stadium with friends.

               ‐   Ask him if he would like to come.

               ‐   Say you have a ticket for him.

               ‐    Tell him you are going to eat in a restaurant later.

                                 OR

      (b)  You are in a school in Alicante for two weeks.
          Write a diary entry, in Spanish, including any four of the following details:

               ‐   Describe what the school is like.

               ‐   Say you are very happy. Explain why.

               ‐   Say you are speaking a lot of Spanish.

               ‐   Mention one thing you don’t like about the school.

               ‐   Say what you do every day.
