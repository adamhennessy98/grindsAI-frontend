# Question

Question 1                                Letter / E‐mail                           (40 marks)

1.   Your Spanish friend Nuria has sent you a present for your birthday.
     Write a letter /e‐mail to her in Spanish.
     Use any five of the points mentioned below:

        ‐   Thank Nuria for the lovely present.
        ‐    Explain how you celebrated your birthday.
        ‐   Describe some presents you received.
        ‐    Tell her you have exams next week and say how you feel.
        ‐   Mention two things you will do after your exams.
        ‐    Invite Nuria to come to Ireland for one week in July.
        ‐   Say what the weather is normally like in Ireland in July.
        ‐   Suggest two things you can do during her visit.

# Marking Scheme

Question 1.  UN VIAJE INOLVIDABLE                                            (50 marks)


All answers must be in Spanish.

Language manipulation is not tested and transcription (relevant and within reasonable limits) is
acceptable. There are no penalties for spelling inaccuracies. Words in brackets are accepted for full
marks but are not essential for correct answer. Marks will be deducted for irrelevant material or for
the omission of material essential to the answer.


        (a)     Original, diferente, inolvidable                                      (3+3+3m)

        (b)    (Porque) se puede ver la naturaleza desde las alturas                         (5m)

        (c)     Tiene más de treinta años de experiencia (ofreciendo viajes en globo)           (5m)

        (d)    Amigos, familiares, parejas (o) compañeros de trabajo                 (2+2+2+2m)

        (e)   One of:  - Es la zona natural protegida más importante de Europa
                                - Se pueden ver los árboles de pinos y olivares, además de varias
                        especies de aves y reptiles                                     (5m)

         (f)    Deben estar en buena salud y no tener miedo a volar                       (5m)


        (g)   Two of:    - Beber alcohol
                                  - Tomar drogas
                                  - Fumar tabaco                                            (4+4m)

        (h)   De lunes a viernes pero no en días festivos                                (5m)
