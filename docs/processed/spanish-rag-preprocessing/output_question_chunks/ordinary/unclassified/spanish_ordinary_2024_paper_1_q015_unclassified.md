# Question

Question 1                                Letter / E-mail                           (40 marks)

     Your friend Clara has invited you to stay with her in Buenos Aires, Argentina.
     Write a letter /e-mail to her in Spanish.

     Use any five of the points mentioned below:

        -   Thank Clara for the invitation to visit her family.
        -   Say you would love to go to Argentina to learn about the culture.
        -   Give her your opinion on the Spanish language.
        -    Tell her you just finished your Spanish exam and explain how it was.
        -   Describe what you did to celebrate the end of the exams.
        -   Mention two activities you would like to do in Buenos Aires.
        -    Tell her you will start a new job in a supermarket in July.
        -   Say what you plan to do next year after the Leaving Certificate.

# Marking Scheme

Question 1.  LA HABANA: CAPITAL DE CUBA                                       (50 marks)

All answers must be in Spanish.



Language manipulation is not tested and transcription (relevant and within reasonable limits) is
acceptable. There are no penalties for spelling inaccuracies. Words in brackets are accepted for full
marks but are not essential for correct answer. Marks will be deducted for irrelevant material or for
the omission of material essential to the answer.



(a)   (Porque) hay interesantes museos, monumentos históricos y coloridos murales         (5m)

(b)    Era el mayor importador de coches estadounidenses                               (5m)

(c)   Pasear a pie por sus calles para admirar los edificios                                (5m)


(d)     (Es una bonita historia sobre) un viejo pescador cubano                            (5m)



(e)   Two of: delfines/estrellas de mar/tortugas marinas (en su hábitat natural)           (3+3m)
