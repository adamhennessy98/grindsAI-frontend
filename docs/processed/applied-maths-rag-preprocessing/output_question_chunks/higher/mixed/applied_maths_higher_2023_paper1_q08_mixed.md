along the horizontal 𝚤⃗ axis. The motion of the balls may be modelled as projectile motion in a
vertical plane, ignoring the effects of air resistance.
𝑃 is projected from point 𝐴 at time 𝑡ൌ0 s with initial velocity 38 m s–1 at 41° to 𝐴𝐵.
𝑄 is projected from point 𝐵 at time 𝑡ൌ1 s with initial velocity 𝑢 m s–1 at 64° to 𝐵𝐴.
