     The company introduces a strategy to attract new customers.
     The company develops a difference equation to predict 𝑆௡, the total number of customers in
    month 𝑛. The difference equation is:
                      𝑆௡ାଶൌ1 ሺ𝑆௡ାଵ൅5𝑆௡ሻെ15𝑛൅40                               4
     where 𝑛൒0, 𝑛∈ℤ.
     Immediately before the introduction of the new strategy, the company has 2016 customers,
        i.e. 𝑆଴ൌ2016. After one month the company has 3500 customers, i.e. 𝑆ଵൌ3500.
(i)   Write down the values of 𝑆ଶ and 𝑆ଷ.
