# Question

2.    (a)  A ship is travelling at 22 km h–1 in a direction west 30° north. A boat sets out to
           intercept the ship from a point 25 km south of the ship.

         The speed of the boat is 55 km h–1.

           Find

                (i)   the direction the boat should steer

                 (ii)   the time, to the nearest minute, that it takes the boat to intercept the ship

                 (iii)  the distance between the boat and the ship 10 minutes before they meet.
