# Question

Question 10
(a)  A small local jewellery business started selling their products online on January 1st 2024.
     They sold nine pieces of jewellery in January and twelve pieces in February.

     With a marketing plan the business predicts a strong growth in online sales. They predict
      that 𝐽, the number of such jewellery pieces sold in any month, will be equal to the number of
      pieces sold the previous month plus twice the number of pieces sold the month before that.

      This prediction can be expressed as the second-order difference equation:
                            𝐽௡ାଶ−𝐽௡ାଵ−2𝐽௡= 0
     where 𝑛≥0, 𝑛∈ℤ, 𝐽଴= 9 and 𝐽ଵ= 12.
      This difference equation has the characteristic quadratic equation 𝑥ଶ−𝑥−2 = 0.


(i)   Solve this quadratic equation, i.e. calculate the two roots of the equation.





(ii)  Hence or otherwise, solve the difference equation to find an expression for 𝐽௡ in terms of 𝑛.

(b)   John’s parents started a college fund for him when he was born in June 2006.
    On his birth date they invested €3000.
     Every year after that, on his birthday, they put €1500 into the fund, which earned a fixed
     annual interest rate of 5.5%.
     The value, 𝑃, in €, of John’s college fund after 𝑛 years may be modelled by the difference
     equation:
                           𝑃௡ାଵ= 1.055𝑃௡+ 1500
     where 𝑛≥0, 𝑛∈ℤ and 𝑃଴= 3000.


(i)   Solve this difference equation to find an expression for 𝑃௡, the value of John’s college fund
      after 𝑛 years.
