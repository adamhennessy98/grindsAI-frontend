# Question

Question 8
(a)   Rugby players are trying to push a 500 kg scrum machine
     along rough horizontal ground.
     The force exerted by the rugby players is horizontal.
     The coefficient of friction between the scrum machine and
     the ground is 0.65.




(i)   Draw a diagram to show the forces acting on the scrum machine.





(ii)   Calculate the vertical normal reaction force on the scrum machine.





(iii)    If the players exert a horizontal force of 3000 N on the scrum machine, will the scrum
     machine move? Give a reason for your answer.
