# Question

Question 6
A car is parked at a point 𝑃. At time 𝑡ൌ0 s the car begins to travel in a straight line with a
constant acceleration of 4.5 m s–2. When the car has reached a velocity of 18 m s–1 it stops
accelerating. The car continues travelling at a velocity of 18 m s–1 until 𝑡ൌ30 s.
(i)    Calculate the time it takes for the car to reach 18 m s–1.





(ii)   Calculate the distance travelled by the car while it is accelerating.
