# Question

1.   The points P and Q lie 1 km apart on a straight level road. A car passes P with a speed of
     2 m s–1 and accelerates with a uniform acceleration of 2.5 m s–2 for 8 seconds to a speed of
     v m s–1.  It then travels at this constant speed of v m s–1 for 18 seconds. Finally, the car
      decelerates uniformly to rest at Q.

      Calculate

        (i)   the speed v

        (ii)   the distance travelled in the first 8 seconds

         (iii)  the distance travelled in the next 18 seconds

       (iv)  the total time it takes the car to travel from P to Q.

      Later, a motorbike passes P with a speed of k m s–1 and continues at this speed for
     10 seconds.  It then accelerates uniformly for a further 2 seconds to a speed of 17 m s–1.
        It continues at this speed until it passes Q. The motorbike takes one minute to travel from
     P to Q.

      (v)  Draw a speed‐time graph of the motion of the motorbike from P to Q.

       (vi)  Find the value of k.
