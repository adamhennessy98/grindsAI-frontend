# Question

7.    In the case of each of the following pairs of terms, clearly distinguish between the first term
     and second term by writing a brief sentence about each.

       (a)   Carpal and tarsal.





      (b)  Tendon and ligament.





       (c)   Biceps and triceps.





      (d)   Osteoblast and osteoclast.





      (e)   Axial skeleton and appendicular skeleton.
