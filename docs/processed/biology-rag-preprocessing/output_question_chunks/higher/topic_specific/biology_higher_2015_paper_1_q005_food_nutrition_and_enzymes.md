# Question

1.    Answer any five of the following parts (a) to (f):

       (a)   What name is given to the simplest units of carbohydrates? ________________________________

       (b)  Name a catabolic process that produces these simplest units. _______________________________

       (c)   The general formula of carbohydrates is Cx(H2O)y.
          What is the most common value of y in the carbohydrates used for energy by human cells?

           ______________________________________________________________________________

       (d)  Name a structural polysaccharide found in plants. _______________________________________

       (e)  Name a polysaccharide, other than the one referred to in part (d), commonly found in plants.

          _______________________________________________________________________________

        (f)   Which carbohydrate is always found in DNA? __________________________________________
