# Marking Scheme

2.                                             2(5) + 5(2)
       (a)  Why nutrients recycled:
          So can be reused or used by other organisms
       (b)   Benefit of N2 changing to nitrates:
           Plants can absorb (or use or avail of) nitrate
        (c)   Nitrate in soil formed directly from:
             Nitrite (NO2-) [allow ammonia]
       (d)   Animal role (not consumer) in N-cycle:
           Excrete (or egest) nitrogen (compounds) or die (or decay or decompose) and release
           nitrogen (compounds) or assimilate nitrogen (compounds) or waste contains nitrogen
          (compounds)
       (e)   Plants in symbiosis with N-fixing bacteria:
         Legumes

            Denitrification or described

           nitrogen (to the soil)
