# Question

15.  Answer any two of (a), (b), (c).                                                             (30, 30)

       (a)     (i)    Explain the term homeostasis.
                  (ii)  Why is homeostasis important in the human body?
                  (iii)  Describe in detail the process of inhalation.
               (iv)  Name a human breathing disorder.
             (v)   State a cause and a treatment for the breathing disorder referred to in part (iv).



      (b)  The vertebral column is part of the human axial skeleton and is composed of bones called
           vertebrae, with intervertebral discs.

                 (i)   There are five regions in the vertebral column. Name any two of these regions and
                 state how many bones are found in each named region.
                  (ii)  What are the intervertebral discs made of and what is their function?
                  (iii)  Bone tissue is continually broken down and reformed during life.
