# Question

9.    (a)  Answer the following in relation to enzymes and enzyme immobilisation.
                (i)    Briefly explain the term enzyme.



                 (ii)   State one advantage of immobilising enzymes.



      (b)   During your practical studies, you prepared one enzyme immobilisation and examined
               its application.
                (i)  Name the enzyme or cell you immobilised.



                 (ii)   Describe the procedure you used to immobilise the enzyme or cell.
              You may include a labelled diagram if you wish.





                 (iii)  Describe how you examined the application of the immobilised enzyme or cell.
