Question 4                                                                           2(5) + 5(2)

     Rectangle correctly indicating one DNA nucleotide showing one phosphate, one deoxyribose
    and one base
 (c) What does DNA stand for?
     Deoxyribonucleic acid
 (d) What type of bonding is represented by the letter X on the diagram?

     Purine
