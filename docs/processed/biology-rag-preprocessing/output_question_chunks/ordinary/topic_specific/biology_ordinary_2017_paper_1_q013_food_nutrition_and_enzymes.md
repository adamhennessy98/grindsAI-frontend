# Question

5.    Indicate whether the following statements are true (T) or false (F) by drawing a circle around
     T or F in each case.

     Example: An enzyme is a biological catalyst                          T            F

       (a)   Enzymes are made from carbohydrates.                         T            F

      (b)  The substance with which an enzyme reacts is called its substrate.   T            F

       (c)   Enzyme reactions are specific.                                 T            F

      (d)   Amylases are enzymes that digest lipids in the body.               T            F

      (e)   Changing pH can affect the rate of activity of an enzyme.           T            F

        (f)   Enzyme molecules have a folded shape.                         T            F

       (g)  An immobilised enzyme is one that has lost its function.            T            F
