# Question

5.    Indicate whether the following statements are true (T) or false (F) by drawing a circle around
     T or F in each case.

     Example: Abiotic factors are non‐living ecological factors.                  T       F

       (a)   The sun is the primary source of energy for our planet.                T       F

      (b)   Pollution is the removal of harmful substances from the environment.    T       F

       (c)   Qualitative surveys record the number of organisms.                  T       F

      (d)  Food chains are always more than five organisms long.                T       F

      (e)   Herbivores are animals that feed on plants only.                      T       F

        (f)   The functional role of an organism is called its niche.                  T       F

       (g)  A producer is always at the start of a food chain.                      T       F
