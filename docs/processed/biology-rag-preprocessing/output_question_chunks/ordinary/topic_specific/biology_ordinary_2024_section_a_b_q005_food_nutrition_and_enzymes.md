# Marking Scheme

1. Key words or terms or phrases may be awarded marks, only if presented in the correct
       context.

      Sample question:           Outline how you quantified a named animal in your habitat
                                    study.

      Marking scheme states:   Named animal / captured / method of capture / counted /
                                  released / recaptured / data recorded / calculation described
                                                            Any four      4(3)

      Sample answer:                     I captured hares using a pooter and counted them.

      Although the candidate has named an animal, mentioned that it was captured, and how
       they caught it, the method of capture is not correct with regard to the animal. The
       candidate’s answer can only be awarded 3(3).
