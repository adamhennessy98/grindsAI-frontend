# Question

2.    Indicate whether the following statements are true (T) or false (F) by drawing a circle around
    T or F in each case.

     Example: A tissue is a group of cells which carry out the same function    T         F

      (a)   Tissues are made of organs.                                 T          F

      (b)   Tissue culture is the growth of tissue in test tubes or Petri dishes.         T          F

      (c)  Human skin tissue will grow best at 10 °C.                       T          F

      (d)   Cells will grow only in a sterile medium.                        T          F

      (e)   Tissue culture is used in micropropagation.                       T          F

       (f)   Cancer is the controlled growth of cells.                         T          F

      (g)  Chemotherapy is used as a cancer treatment.                      T          F
