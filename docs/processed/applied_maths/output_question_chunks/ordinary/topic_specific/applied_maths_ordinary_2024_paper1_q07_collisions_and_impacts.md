# Question

Question 7
A small smooth sphere, 𝐴, of mass 4 kg travels with a constant speed of 5 m s–1.
It collides with another small smooth sphere, 𝐵, of mass 6 kg travelling in the same direction with
a speed of 2.5 m s–1.
The coefficient of restitution between the spheres is 𝑒.

                           5 m s–1                2.5 m s–1


                        𝐴               𝐵


After impact, sphere 𝐵 continues to travel in the same direction, but with a speed of 4 m s–1.


(i)    Calculate the speed of 𝐴 after impact.
