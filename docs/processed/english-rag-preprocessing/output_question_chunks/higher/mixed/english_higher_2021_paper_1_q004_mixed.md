# Question

QUESTION B – 40 Marks

You have been invited to write a feature article, entitled Monumental Matters – The Story of Statues,
to appear in the magazine supplement of a weekend newspaper. In your article you should: reflect on
the long-established tradition of erecting statues to celebrate or memorialise people, explore some of
the reasons why commemorative statues may be controversial, and give your views on continuing this
tradition into the future.
