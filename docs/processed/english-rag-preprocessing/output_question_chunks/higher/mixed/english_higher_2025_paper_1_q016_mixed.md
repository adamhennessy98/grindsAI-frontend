# Marking Scheme

7.    In TEXT 3, the character Roman thinks that – “A human being was not made to stand still.”

     Write a short story featuring an ambitious character whose reckless actions lead to disaster.

       Candidates should write a short story featuring an ambitious character whose reckless
       actions lead to disaster. Allow for a variety of approaches to the task. Allow a broad
       interpretation of ‘ambitious’. Reward demonstration of competence in the controlled
      use of features of the genre.
