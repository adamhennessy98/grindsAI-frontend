# Question

2.   Compare how effectively at least one technique is employed, by the authors of at least
     two texts on your comparative course, to manipulate the reader’s emotional response to
      these texts. Develop your response with reference to your chosen texts.
     You may refer to the same or different techniques in relation to each of your
      chosen texts.                                                                    (70)
