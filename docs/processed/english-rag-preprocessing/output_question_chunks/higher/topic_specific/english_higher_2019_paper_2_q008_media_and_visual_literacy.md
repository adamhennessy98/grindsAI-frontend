# Question

1.   Brendan Kennelly

      Discuss how Kennelly’s sensitive exploration of a range of emotions, and his imaginative
      use of a variety of characters, help to reveal the humanity intrinsic to his work. Develop
      your response with reference to the poems by Brendan Kennelly on your course.

# Marking Scheme

1.     In TEXT 2, Tom expresses the view that people favour photographs that feature sunsets.

     Write a descriptive essay which captures a sense of the difference between dawn and dusk
     and celebrates both the beginning and the end of the day.

Candidates should write a descriptive essay which captures a sense of the difference between
dawn and dusk and celebrates both the beginning and the end of the day. A descriptive essay may
contain other elements (e.g. narrative) but the focus should be placed on descriptive writing.

Mark ex 100 by reference to the criteria for assessment.
