# Question

2.     Eiléan Ní Chuilleanáin

        “Eiléan Ní Chuilleanáin tells fascinating stories, often examining themes that are
        relevant to contemporary Ireland, in a style that is both beautiful and mysterious.”

       To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement? Support your answer with
        reference to the poetry of Eiléan Ní Chuilleanáin on your course.
