# Question

1.     (a)   Discuss the extent to which the level of freedom enjoyed by a central character
             in one text on your comparative course is determined by this character’s social
            position and status.
          Develop your response with reference to your chosen text.               (30)

        (b)  In the case of two other texts you have studied on your comparative course,
          compare the extent to which the level of freedom enjoyed by a central character
             in each of these texts is determined by these characters’ social position and
             status.
          Develop your response with reference to your chosen texts.              (40)
