# Question

5.   Adrienne Rich

     “A powerful sense of sadness is conveyed in Rich’s poetry through her exposure of the
      flawed nature of our existence.”

     To what extent do you agree or disagree with the above statement? You should refer to
      both the language and the themes in the poetry by Adrienne Rich on your Leaving
       Certificate English course.
