# Question

6.     Bono refers to “... telling the secrets of the age ...” in TEXT 1.

       Write a short story in which a closely guarded secret is gradually revealed.

# Marking Scheme

6.  Bono refers to “... telling the secrets of the age ...” in TEXT 1.

Write a short story in which a closely guarded secret is gradually revealed.

Reward awareness of the narrative shape of a short story (setting, characterisation, plot,
dialogue, tension, etc.) and the quality of the writing. The story should involve the gradual
disclosure of a closely guarded secret.
