# Question

2.    Eiléan Ní Chuilleanáin

       “Ní Chuilleanáin uses evocative imagery to produce poems that often reveal
      meaningful insights about the nature of life.”

       Discuss this statement developing your response with reference to the poetry by Eiléan
       Ní Chuilleanáin on your Leaving Certificate English course.

# Marking Scheme
