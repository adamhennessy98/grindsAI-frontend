# Question

3.   W.B. Yeats

       “Yeats’s poetry is both intellectually stimulating and emotionally charged.”

      Discuss the extent to which you agree or disagree with the above statement. Develop
      your response with reference to the themes and language evident in the poems by
     W. B. Yeats on your course.
