# Question

5.       In TEXT 1, Shakespeare’s characters encounter many adventures on their travels.

       Write a speech, for a class debate for or against the motion: “Young people should travel
       and see the world before joining the workforce or furthering their education.”

# Marking Scheme

5.  In TEXT 1, Shakespeare’s characters encounter many adventures on their travels.

see the world before joining the workforce or furthering their education.”

Reward a clearly established register, appropriate to a speech to be delivered to classmates.
