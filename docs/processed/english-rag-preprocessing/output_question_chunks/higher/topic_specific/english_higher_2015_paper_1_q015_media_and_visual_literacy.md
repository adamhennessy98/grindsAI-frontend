# Question

4.       In TEXT 2, Joanna Briscoe, writing about fiction, tells us that “Endings can be a problem.”

       Write a personal essay about your response to an ending, or endings, in your
           life that you consider significant.

# Marking Scheme

4.  In TEXT 2, Joanna Briscoe, writing about fiction, tells us that “Endings can be a problem.”

Write a personal essay about your response to an ending, or endings, in your life that you
consider significant.

Reward a clearly established and sustained register appropriate to a personal essay. Candidates
may adopt a variety of approaches (serious, humorous, anecdotal, discursive, etc.), but they
should include a reflective element, focus on one or more significant “endings” in their lives
and their response to them. Allow for a broad interpretation of “endings”.
