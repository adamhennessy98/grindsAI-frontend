# Question

5.       In TEXT 3, Penelope Lively writes that she sometimes feels like an “observant time-traveller”.

       Write a descriptive essay which captures life in Ireland in 2015 from the point of view
        of an observant time-traveller. The time-traveller may be from the past or from the
        future.

# Marking Scheme

5.  In TEXT 3, Penelope Lively writes that she sometimes feels like an “observant time-
     traveller”.

Write a descriptive essay which captures life in Ireland in 2015 from the point of view of
an observant time-traveller. The time-traveller may be from the past or from the future.

Candidates may choose to adopt various approaches (personal, anecdotal, humorous,
informative, aesthetic, etc.). They should write in a descriptive style, to capture life in Ireland
in 2015 from the point of view of an observant time-traveller from the past or from the future.
Reward the quality of the description. Allow for a broad interpretation of “time-traveller”.
