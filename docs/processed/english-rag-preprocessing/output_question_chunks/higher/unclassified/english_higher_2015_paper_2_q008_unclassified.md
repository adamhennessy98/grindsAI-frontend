# Question

1.    John Montague

      “Montague makes effective use of evocative language to express a profound empathy
       with others.”

       Discuss this statement, supporting your answer with reference to the poetry of John
      Montague on your course.

# Marking Scheme

1.  TEXTS 1, 2 and 3 deal with the theme of challenges.

Write a short story in which the main character is transformed when faced with a daunting
challenge.

Reward awareness of the narrative shape of a short story (setting, characterisation, plot,
dialogue, tension, etc.) and the quality of the writing. A main character should undergo a
transformation when faced with a daunting challenge. Allow for a broad interpretation of
“character is transformed” and “daunting challenge”.
