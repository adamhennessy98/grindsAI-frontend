# Marking Scheme

4.    TEXT 3 offers a thought‐provoking depiction of soldiers and their leaders.

     Write a discursive essay in which you consider the subject of leaders and leadership.

Expect candidates to write a discursive essay in which they consider the subject of leaders and
leadership. Allow for a broad interpretation of both “leaders” and “leadership”, and for a
variety of approaches to the task.
