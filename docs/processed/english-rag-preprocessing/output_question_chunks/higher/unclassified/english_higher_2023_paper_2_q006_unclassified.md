# Question

1.    (a)   In the case of one text on your comparative course, discuss how contradictory
           aspects of human nature emerge from a study of a particular theme or issue.
          Develop your response with reference to your chosen text.                     (30)

       (b)  Compare the extent to which contradictory aspects of human nature emerge
          from a study of the same theme or issue discussed in 1. (a) above, in two other
            texts on your comparative course. Develop your response with reference to your
          chosen texts.                                                                (40)
