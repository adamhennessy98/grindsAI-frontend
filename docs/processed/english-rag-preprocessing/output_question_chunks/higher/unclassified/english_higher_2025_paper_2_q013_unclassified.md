# Question

4.   Derek Mahon

      To what extent do you think an audience’s appreciation of both Mahon’s language and
      thematic concerns would be enhanced if his poetry was read aloud?

      Develop your response with reference to the poetry of Derek Mahon on your Leaving
       Certificate English course.
