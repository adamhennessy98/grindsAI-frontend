# Marking Scheme

7.    TEXT 1 contains the statement: “Language is a great weapon”.

     You are competing in the final of a national public speaking competition. The topic to be
     addressed is: Language is a great weapon. You are free to agree or disagree. Write the
     speech you would deliver.

Expect candidates to write a speech, suitable for delivery at the final of a national public
speaking competition, on the topic: Language is a great weapon. Allow for a variety of
approaches to the task: informative, persuasive, discursive, argumentative, reflective, etc.



Mark ex 100 by reference to the criteria for assessment.
