# Question

2.    “A variety of factors in texts can change or reinforce our initial impression of the general
        vision and viewpoint.”

      Compare the main factor or factors in three texts on your comparative course that changed
       or reinforced your initial impression of the general vision and viewpoint in these texts.  (70)
