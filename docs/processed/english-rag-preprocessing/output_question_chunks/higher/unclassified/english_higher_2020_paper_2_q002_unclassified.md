# Question

1.     (a)   Identify and discuss two aspects of cultural context which you believe make a
              significant contribution to the level of social division or the level of social unity
            evident in one text on your comparative course. Develop your response with
            reference to the text.                                                    (30)

        (b)  Compare the extent to which social division is evident in each of two other
           comparative texts you have studied. Develop your response with reference
            to the aspect or aspects of cultural context that you believe contribute(s) to
           the level of social division evident in these texts.

             In your answer you may refer to the same aspect or different aspects of
             cultural context in each of the texts that you have studied.               (40)
