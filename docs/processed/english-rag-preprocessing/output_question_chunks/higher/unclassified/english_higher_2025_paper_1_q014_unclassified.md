# Question

5.    In TEXT 2 Margaret Atwood tells young people, “you’ve got the energy and optimism to take on
       the challenge.”

       Write a feature article entitled, ‘A love letter to Ireland’ in which you explore some of the
       reasons why we should view contemporary Irish society through an optimistic lens.
