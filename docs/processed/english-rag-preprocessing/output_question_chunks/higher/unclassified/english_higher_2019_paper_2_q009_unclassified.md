# Question

2.    Elizabeth Bishop

      “Bishop makes skillful use of a variety of poetic techniques to produce poems that are
      often analytical but rarely emotional.”

       Discuss the extent to which you agree or disagree with the above statement. Develop
      your response with reference to the poems by Elizabeth Bishop on your course.
