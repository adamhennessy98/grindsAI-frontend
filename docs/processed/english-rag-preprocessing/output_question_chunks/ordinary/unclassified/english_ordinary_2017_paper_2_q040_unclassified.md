# Marking Scheme

2. The theme of TEXTS 1, 2 and 3 is School Days.

Imagine that you have been selected as the Student of the Year in your school and have
been asked to deliver a speech to the staff and students at a school assembly. The topic
for your speech is “My School Days”. Write the speech, which may be serious or
humorous or both, that you would deliver.

Candidates should write a speech on the topic of “My School Days”. Expect the writing to be
shaped as a speech. The tone and register may be formal or informal but should be suitable
for the intended audience of school staff and students. Expect that candidates will respond
in a variety of ways. Consideration should be given to the quality of all aspects of the
composition.
