# Question

3.   You are giving a talk to your class about the powerful mood created by Doerr in his
      novel, All the Light We Cannot See.
       In your talk you should:
        -   Describe two moments in the novel when the mood was particularly powerful
        -   Explain why the mood was so powerful in your chosen moments
        -   Explain what your overall feelings were when you finished studying the novel.
      Your response should demonstrate your knowledge of the text.
