# Question

2.   Answer ONE of the following: [Each part carries 20 marks]

         (i)   You have been asked to paint a picture based on one of the poems on your Leaving
             Certificate English course. Explain why you would or would not choose the poem,
           Jungian Cows, for this purpose. Your answer should demonstrate your
           understanding of the poem.
                              OR

          (ii)  Do you think the poet makes effective use of language to convey a vivid
           impression of the man who works the machine to milk the cows in the above
        poem? Support your response, with reference to the poet’s use of language for
             this purpose in the poem, Jungian Cows.

                              OR

          (iii)   Explain why you would or would not include the poem, Jungian Cows, in a
            collection of poetry about the relationship between humans and the animal
           world. Support your answer with reference to the poem.
