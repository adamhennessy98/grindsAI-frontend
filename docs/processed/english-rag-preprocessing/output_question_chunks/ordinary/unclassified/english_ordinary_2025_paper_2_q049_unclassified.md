# Question

2.   Answer ONE of the following: [Each part carries 20 marks]

         (i)   As a revision exercise, before the Leaving Certificate examination, your English teacher
     has asked you to deliver a brief presentation to the class about the poem you believe you will
         still remember in twenty years’ time. Write the text of your presentation, using ‘This Moment’
     by Eavan Boland as your chosen poem. Support your response with reference to ‘This Moment’.
     Your response should demonstrate your knowledge of the poem.
                                 OR
          (ii)   There has been an ongoing debate on a popular radio call‐in show about whether or not
      students of English should continue to study poetry in the future. Some callers have been
      arguing that there is little to be gained from studying poetry and that more relevant things
      should be taught in English class today. You disagree with this view and decide to call in, using
       ‘This Moment’ by Eavan Boland to support your opinions. Your response should
     demonstrate your knowledge of the poem
                                 OR
         (iii)  The imagery Eavan Boland uses in her poem ‘This Moment’ helps the reader to
     understand the interesting themes in the poem. Do you agree or disagree with this view?
     Support your response with reference to ‘This Moment’. Your response should demonstrate
     your knowledge of the poem.





    Leaving Certificate Examination 2025                 16
    English – Ordinary Level – Paper 2
