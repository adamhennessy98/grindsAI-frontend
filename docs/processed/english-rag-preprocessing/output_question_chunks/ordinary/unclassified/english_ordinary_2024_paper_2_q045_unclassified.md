PRESCRIBED POETRY (50 marks)
  You must answer on ONE of the following poems: (A - F)

A       NIGHT DRIVE

 The closest, Mother, we have been in years           in your lap, bracing yourself for the impact,
 was a night drive back from Achill on our own.       hearing you whisper ‘Jesus’ under your breath,
 Our tyres pressed their smooth cheeks to the ice,    preparing your soul for the moment of death.
 gripping nothing, squealing, barely holding on.       Then, just as suddenly, nothing happened –

 Something stepped into our beam and stood there,  the sheep stepped back into the verge
 dumbly, ready to confront its death.                  for no reason, attracted by a clump of grass.
  I remember your right hand in the darkness –        For days I felt the pressure of your hand on mine.
 a white bird frightened from its fastness           You would’ve led me to the next world, Mother, like a child.

                                                                Tom French
