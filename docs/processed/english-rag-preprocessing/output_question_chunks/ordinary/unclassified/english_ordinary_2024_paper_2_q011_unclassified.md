# Question

2.    Identify three objects, featured or referred to, in Friel’s play, Philadelphia, Here I
      Come!, that you believe helped you to understand a key aspect of the play.
       In the case of each object, explain how it helped you to understand a key aspect
       of the play. Your response should demonstrate your knowledge of the text.
