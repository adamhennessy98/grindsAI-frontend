# Question

6.    In TEXT 3, Tatty’s father briefly loses his daughter.

        Write a personal essay in which you reflect on various significant losses in your
             life.

# Marking Scheme

6.  In TEXT 3, Tatty’s father briefly loses his daughter.
   Write a personal essay in which you reflect on various significant losses in
   your life.

Candidates should write a personal essay in which they write in a reflective manner
about various significant losses in their lives. Expect that the personal essay will be
from the perspective of the engaged ‘I’ – the register personal, etc.  It is envisaged
that candidates will adopt a wide variety of approaches to framing their views.
Allow for a broad interpretation of “significant losses”. Consideration should be
given to the quality of all aspects of the composition.
