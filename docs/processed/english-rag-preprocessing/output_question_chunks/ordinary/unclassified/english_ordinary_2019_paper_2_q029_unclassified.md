# Question

1.     (a)  (i)  Name one of your three comparative texts and identify a relationship in it.
            Use one or more key moments to explain how the characters in this relationship
               did or did not support each other when faced with a problem, challenge or crisis
                 in the text. Support your answer with reference to the text.                (15)

                 (ii) Name another comparative text you have studied and identify a relationship
                 in it. Use one or more key moments to explain how the characters in this
                relationship did or did not support each other when faced with a problem,
               challenge or crisis in the text. Support your answer with reference to the text.
                                                                                           (15)

        (b)   Relationships often feature one main emotion (e.g. love, hatred, jealousy, etc.).
          Choose a relationship from each of at least two comparative texts you have
            studied, and compare these relationships with reference to the main emotion
            evident in each. Support your answer with reference to your chosen texts.  (40)
