# Question

5.    In TEXT 1, we read how Lady Gregory was visited by her famous friends, Shaw and Yeats,
       at her home in County Clare.

      Write a short story in which the close friendship between two life-long friends comes
      under pressure when one of the friends becomes rich and famous.
