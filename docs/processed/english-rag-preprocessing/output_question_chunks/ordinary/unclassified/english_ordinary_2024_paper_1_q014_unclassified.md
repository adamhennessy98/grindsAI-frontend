2.    In TEXT 3 Martin Luther King Jr. advises people ‘to face the challenge of change’.

     Write a short story, featuring a character who has to overcome difficulties because of a
      significant change in his or her life.

    Candidates should write a short story about a character who has to overcome difficulties
   because of a significant change in his or her life.

    Consideration should be given to the quality of all aspects of the composition with
    regard to the Criteria for Assessment.


   Mark ex 100 by reference to the Criteria for Assessment.


    P:   Focus – a short story about a character who has to overcome difficulties
        because of a significant change in his or her life.
         Understanding of genre – the writing should be shaped as a short story

           line; a defining moment of experience followed by a change; a resolution, etc.
