# Marking Scheme

5.   In TEXT 3, Kailash Satyarthi observes that, “Time is running out.”

     Write a short story which involves a race against time to prevent a disaster.

Candidates should write a short story which involves a race against time to prevent a
disaster – allow for a broad interpretation of “disaster”. The writing should be shaped as
a short story having some sense of a beginning – middle – end; a central character; a time‐
line; a defining moment of experience followed by a change; a resolution, etc. Expect that
candidates will respond in a variety of ways. Consideration should be given to the quality
of all aspects of the composition.
