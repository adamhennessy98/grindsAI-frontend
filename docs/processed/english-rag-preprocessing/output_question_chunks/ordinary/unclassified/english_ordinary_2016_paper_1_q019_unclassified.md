# Marking Scheme

5.   Chris and Anna learn about many different aspects of China while on
     their travels in TEXT 1.

  Write an article for a travel magazine, in which you encourage young
  people to visit Ireland, telling them why they should come here, and what
  you recommend they should see and do during their visit.

Candidates should write an article, suitable for publication in a travel magazine
and appealing to a youthful audience. In it they should encourage young people to
visit Ireland, telling them why they should come here and what they recommend they
should see and do during their visit. An article can be discursive/persuasive/
anecdotal/an opinion piece/ a ‘colour’ piece. Layout may include headings, sub-
headings, etc. Language register(s) may be a little relaxed, appropriate to a broad
audience, etc. Consideration should be given to the quality of all aspects of the
composition.
