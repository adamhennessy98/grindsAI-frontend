    what you think you, and young people generally, could do to help build better lives

Candidates should write an article in which they outline what they think young people could
do to help build better lives for people in their community, and in the world generally. The
tone and register may be formal or informal but should be suitable for the intended
audience. The two elements required in the response should be present, although not
necessarily receiving equal attention. Expect the writing to be shaped as an article.
