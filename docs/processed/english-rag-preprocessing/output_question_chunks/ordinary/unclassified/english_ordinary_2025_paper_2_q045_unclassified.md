PRESCRIBED POETRY (50 marks)

   You must answer on ONE of the following poems: (A ‐ F)

A                             Genetics

 My father’s in my fingers, but my mother’s in my palms.     I shape a chapel where a steeple stands.
   I lift them up and look at them with pleasure –         And when I turn it over,
   I know my parents made me by my hands.           my father’s by my fingers, my mother’s by my palms

 They may have been repelled to separate lands,         demure before a priest reciting psalms.
  to separate hemispheres, may sleep with other lovers,   My body is their marriage register.
  but in me they touch where fingers link to palms.                I re‐enact their wedding with my hands.

 With nothing left of their togetherness but friends       So take me with you, take up the skin’s demands
 who quarry for their image by a river,                      for mirroring in bodies of the future.
  at least I know their marriage by my hands.                          I’ll bequeath my fingers, if you bequeath your palms.
                                    We know our parents make us by our hands.

                                                                                      Sinéad Morrissey
