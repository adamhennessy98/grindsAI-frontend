# Question

4.   Your class is having a debate about the ending of Hansberry’s play, A Raisin in the Sun.
       Write the text of your contribution to the debate, beginning either with the line:
                                   I was satisfied with the ending of ‘A Raisin in the Sun’, because …
                                                or
                                I was not satisfied with the ending of ‘A Raisin in the Sun’, because ...
      Argue three points in your response.

      Your response should demonstrate your knowledge of the text.
