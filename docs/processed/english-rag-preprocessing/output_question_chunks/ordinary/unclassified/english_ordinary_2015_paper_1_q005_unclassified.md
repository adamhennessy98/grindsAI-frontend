# Marking Scheme

5.  George and his family try to take good care of their pet birds in TEXT 1.
   Imagine you are a representative of the Irish Society for the Prevention of
   Cruelty to Animals (ISPCA). Write a speech, to be delivered in schools,

   keeping a pet and encourage the students in your audience to treat

Candidates should write a speech as if they are a representative of the ISPCA.
Expect the writing to be shaped as a speech. Candidates should deal with both
aspects of the task: (i) explaining the practicalities and responsibilities involved in
keeping a pet and (ii) encouraging students to treat animals with respect. The tone
and register may be a little relaxed and/or informal given the youthful audience.
Expect that candidates will respond in a variety of ways. Consideration should be
given to the quality of all aspects of the composition.
