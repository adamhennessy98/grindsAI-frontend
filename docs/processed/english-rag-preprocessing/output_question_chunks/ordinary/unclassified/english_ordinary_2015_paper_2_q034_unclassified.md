The article is directed towards students, via a school magazine. The register may
be formal/ casual/ playful/ sarcastic etc. There are three tasks required in the
response, although not necessarily requiring equal attention: (i) why the candidate
likes reading – the type of material is not specified: (ii) a discussion on some of the
books most enjoyed, and (iii) an encouragement to read more. Consideration
should be given to the quality of all aspects of the composition.
