# Question

4.   Your class is having a debate about the ending of Keane’s play, Sive. Write the text of
       your contribution to the debate, beginning either with the line:
                                              I was satisfied with the ending of ‘Sive’, because …
                                                or
                                           I was not satisfied with the ending of ‘Sive’, because ...
      Argue three points in your response.

       Your response should demonstrate your knowledge of the text.
