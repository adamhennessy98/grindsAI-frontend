PRESCRIBED POETRY (50 marks)

You must answer on ONE of the following poems: (A ‐ D)

 A                       BEGIN

 Begin again to the summoning birds          Begin to the loneliness that cannot end
 to the sight of the light at the window,        since it perhaps is what makes us begin,
 begin to the roar of morning traffic           begin to wonder at unknown faces
  all along Pembroke Road.                     at crying birds in the sudden rain
 Every beginning is a promise                  at branches stark in the willing sunlight
 born in light and dying in dark                 at seagulls foraging for bread
 determination and exaltation of springtime   at couples sharing a sunny secret
 flowering the way to work.                  alone together while making good.
 Begin to the pageant of queuing girls        Though we live in a world that dreams of ending
 the arrogant loneliness of swans in the canal  that always seems about to give in
 bridges linking the past and future           something that will not acknowledge conclusion
 old friends passing through with us still.        insists that we forever begin.
                                                            Brendan Kennelly
