# Marking Scheme

4.   TEXTS 1, 2, and 3 all deal with aspects of social media.

     Write a personal essay in which you discuss what you think your life would be
      like if you were unable to use any form of social media for a whole year.

Candidates should write a personal essay in which they discuss what they think their
lives would be like if they were unable to use any form of social media for a whole year.

Consideration should be given to the quality of all aspects of the composition with
regard to the Criteria for Assessment.

Mark ex 100 by reference to the Criteria for Assessment.


P:   Focus – a personal essay in which candidates discuss what they think their lives
    would be like if they were unable to use any form of social media for a whole
     year
