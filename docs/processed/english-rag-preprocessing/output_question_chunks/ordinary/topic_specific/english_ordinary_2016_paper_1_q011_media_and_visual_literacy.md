# Marking Scheme

3.  TEXTS 1, 2 and 3 all feature or refer to diary entries.

    Imagine that it is the year 3016 and you are living in a city on Mars.
    Write three diary entries in which you describe the world in which you
      live, give details of your daily life and your hopes for the future.

Candidates should write three diary entries about their life on Mars. The three
aspects required need to be addressed but not necessarily equally; description of
their world, their daily life and their hopes for the future. Candidates should write
in diary format from the ‘I’ perspective – the register personal, etc. Expect
elements of narrative and descriptive language.  Consideration should be given to
the quality of all aspects of the composition.
