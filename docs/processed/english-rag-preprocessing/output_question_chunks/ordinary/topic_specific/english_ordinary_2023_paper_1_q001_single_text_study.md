# Question

Section I COMPREHENDING and Section II COMPOSING.
  •  The paper contains three texts on the general theme of LIFE LESSONS.
  •  Candidates should familiarise themselves with each of the texts before
     beginning their answers.
  •  Both sections of this paper (COMPREHENDING and COMPOSING) must
     be attempted.
  •  Each section carries 100 marks.

             SECTION I – COMPREHENDING

  • Two questions, A and B, follow each text.
  •  Candidates must answer a Question A on one text and a Question B on
     a different text. Candidates must answer only one Question A and only
     one Question B.
  •  N.B. Candidates may NOT answer a Question A and a Question B on the
    same text.

               SECTION II – COMPOSING
  •  Candidates must write on one of the compositions 1 – 7.
              Do not hand this up.
         This document will not be returned to the
              State Examinations Commission.
