# Marking Scheme

2.   In TEXT 1, Denise Chaila says, “this is who I am – when it comes to gender, when it
    comes to race, when it comes to identity and culture.”

     Write a personal essay in which you reflect on what contributes to your sense
     of identity and who you are.

Candidates should write in a reflective manner about what contributes to their sense
of identity and who they are.

Consideration should be given to the quality of all aspects of the composition with regard
to the Criteria for Assessment.

Mark ex 100 by reference to the Criteria for Assessment.


P: Focus – a personal essay in which candidates reflect on what contributes to their
   sense of identity and who they are.
   Understanding of genre – the writing should be shaped as a personal essay e.g.
    written from the perspective of the engaged ‘I’/personal register/ written in the first
    person/reflective insights/confessional tone/individual observation/use of personal
   anecdotes/ includes pertinent personal details/strongly held personal views and
    opinions/use of authentic personal voice, etc.
