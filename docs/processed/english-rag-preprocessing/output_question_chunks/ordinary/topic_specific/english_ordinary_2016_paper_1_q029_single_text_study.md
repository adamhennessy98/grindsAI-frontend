# Marking Scheme

4.   Kindness is mentioned in TEXT 2.

    Write a personal essay about your own experience of any two of the
     following: kindness; patience; generosity; tolerance and forgiveness.

Candidates should write a personal essay about their own experience of any two of
the following: kindness; patience; generosity; tolerance and forgiveness. Expect
that the personal essay will be from the perspective of the engaged ‘I’ – the register
personal, etc.  It is envisaged that candidates will adopt a wide variety of
approaches to framing their views. Consideration should be given to the quality of
all aspects of the composition.
