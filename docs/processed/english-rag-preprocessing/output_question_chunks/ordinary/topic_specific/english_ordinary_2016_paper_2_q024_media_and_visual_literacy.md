# Marking Scheme

2.   Chris and Anna go on an exciting journey in TEXT 1.

    Write a short story in which the main character goes on an exciting
     journey and learns some valuable lessons along the way.

Candidates should write a short story in which the main character goes on an
exciting journey, and learns some valuable lessons along the way. The writing
should be shaped as a short story having some sense of a beginning – middle – end;
a central character; a time-line; a defining moment or moments of experience
followed by a change; a resolution, etc. Expect that candidates will respond in a
variety of ways. Consideration should be given to the quality of all aspects of the
composition.
