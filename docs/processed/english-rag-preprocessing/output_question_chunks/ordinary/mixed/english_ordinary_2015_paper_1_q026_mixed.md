# Marking Scheme

7.  In TEXT 2, we are told that Brendan O’Carroll’s mother was not entirely
   comfortable with modern devices.
   Write a short story in which one or more modern devices make life
    difficult for the character or characters in the story.

Candidates should write a short story in which modern devices make life difficult for
the character or characters. The writing should be shaped as a short story having
some sense of a beginning – middle – end: a time-line: a defining moment of
experience followed by a change: a resolution, etc. Expect that candidates will
respond in a variety of ways. Allow for a broad interpretation of “modern devices”.
Consideration should be given to the quality of all aspects of the composition.
