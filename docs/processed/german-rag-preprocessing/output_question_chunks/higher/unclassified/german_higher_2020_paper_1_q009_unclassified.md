# Question

1.  Sie sehen unten Sätze, in denen Nomen/Substantive unterstrichen sind. Sehen Sie sich das
    Beispiel an. Geben Sie nun für die anderen fünf Nomen/Substantive an:

   •  ob Singular oder Plural
   •  bei Singular das Geschlecht (feminin, maskulin, neutrum)
   •  den Fall (Nominativ, Akkusativ, Dativ, Genitiv)

     Beispiel: Ich konnte Lenz keine Antwort geben.

                                        Singular, feminin, Akkusativ

     (1)  Am letzten Schultag bekam ich mein Zeugnis.

    _____________________________________________________________________________

    _____________________________________________________________________________

     (3)  Meine Mutter stellte erstaunt ihre Kaffeetasse hin.

# Marking Scheme

1.  (12 marks: (i) 8 marks: 4 x 2 marks; (ii) 4 marks: 4 x 1 mark)

      (i)   Herr Hees runs an unusual school. Give details.
    (Any four: 4 x 2 marks)
     (a)    It’s a dance school/School of Dance
     (b)    It’s 27 years old//It opened in 1993
     (c)   (opened it) when he finished his dance (teaching) education
     (d)    It’s situated in Trierer Straße/in Aachen
     (e)    It’s in a former garage/car repairs place/car repairs workshop

      (ii)  Why did Herr Hees decide to open this type of school? Give details.
    (Any four: 4 x 1 mark)
     (a)  He grew up with dance
     (b)   His parents were both professional/world-famous dancers
     (c)  He did his first dance course when he was 8 years old
     (d)  He has taken part in many competitions
     (e)  He has won many competitions
      (f)   The lifestyle has always interested him
