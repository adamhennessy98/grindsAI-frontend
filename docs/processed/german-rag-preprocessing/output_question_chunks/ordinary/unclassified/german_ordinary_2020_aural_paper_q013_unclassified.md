# Question

        (a)        i siopa a ligeann rothair ar cíos             (a)    a bike rental shop
        (b)        i stáisiún póilíní                             (b)    a police station
         (c)        i siopa báicéara                                (c)    a bakery
        (d)        i siopa éadaí                                (d)    a clothes shop

    Cuir a, b, c, nó d sa bhosca chun do rogha        Indicate your choice by putting a, b, c, or d in
    a chur in iúl                                  the box provided.

Cuid a Ceathair                                   Fourth Part

Anois cloisfidh tú trí mhír nuachta ón raidió     You will now hear three news items taken
agus réamhaisnéis na haimsire ina ndiaidh.     from radio, followed by the weather forecast.
Seinnfear na ceithre mhír faoi thrí. Ar dtús,     The four items will be played three times:
seinnfear tríd síos iad; ansin seinnfear ina       the first time right through, then in four
gceithre mhír iad agus beidh sos ann tar éis     segments with pauses, and finally right
gach míre. Ar deireadh, seinnfear tríd síos an   through again. Answer in English.
athuair iad. Freagair i nGaeilge.
