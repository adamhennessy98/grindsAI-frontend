# Question

9.    (a)  To determine the biochemical oxygen demand (BOD) of the water in a lake, two
          samples were taken and one was tested immediately.
               (i)   Under what conditions was the second sample stored?
               (ii)   After what time interval was it tested?                                           (9)
         The result of the BOD test suggested that eutrophication had occurred in the lake.
                (iii)  Define eutrophication and state one of its harmful effects.
              (iv)  Suggest a possible cause of eutrophication.                                      (9)

      (b)  Why is there concern about lead in drinking water?
        Name an instrumental method used to analyse water for heavy metals.
       How can lead be removed from a contaminated water supply?                      (12)

      (c)  How does temporary hardness arise in rainwater flowing over limestone?             (6)
               (i)   Write a balanced equation to describe the formation of limescale when this
                water is boiled.
               (ii)   Explain in words or by balanced equations how this water with temporary
                hardness is deionised using a cation-exchange resin (RH) and an anion-exchange
                  resin (R*OH).                                                             (14)
