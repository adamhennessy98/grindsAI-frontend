                  (ii)   Account for the increase in electronegativity values across the second period

                  Select, giving your reasons, which of these angles is the most probable value

      (b)   Consider the equilibrium represented by the following balanced equation.
                                   2BrCl (g) ⇌ Br2 (g) +  Cl2 (g)

                 (i)   Write the equilibrium constant (Kc) expression for this reaction.                 (6)

                  (ii)   Calculate the percentage decomposition into bromine and chlorine
                 of 0.200 moles of BrCl, placed initially in a 5 litre closed container, and
               allowed to reach equilibrium at 1200 C according to the equation above.
               Take the value of Kc at this temperature as 0.220.
                Give your answer correct to the nearest whole number.                      (12)
                  (iii)   Increasing the pressure on this equilibrium mixture at 1200 C, intensifies
