                4KNO3 (s) + 5S (s)  2K2O (s) + 2N2 (g) + 5SO2 (g)
        One of the two reactants is in stoichiometric excess when 20.2 g of potassium
             nitrate is mixed with 24.0 g of sulfur.

               (i)   Which reactant is in excess?
