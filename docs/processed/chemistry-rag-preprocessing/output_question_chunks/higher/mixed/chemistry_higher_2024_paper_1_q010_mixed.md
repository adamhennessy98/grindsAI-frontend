# Question

8.   Study the reaction scheme below and answer the questions that follow.

                      B                 A
              C2H5Cl                   C2H6                    C2H4


                                                                                Cl2


                                                    X


                                                                      elimination


                                                        Y


                                                                    polymerisation


                                                    PVC

      (a)  How does the geometry around the carbon atoms change during reaction A?               (5)

     Reaction B takes place in the presence of chlorine (Cl2) gas and ultraviolet light.

      (b)    (i)   Should reaction B be classified as an addition reaction, a substitution reaction or an
                 elimination reaction?

                  (ii)   Describe the mechanism for reaction B.

                  (iii)  A trace quantity of C4H10 is formed during reaction B. Explain how the formation of
                C4H10 is evidence for the mechanism described.

               (iv)  Apart from the formation of C4H10, state one other piece of evidence for this
              mechanism.                                                                       (21)

       (c)   Polyvinylchloride (PVC) is synthesised in three steps from C2H4. These steps are an addition
           reaction to form compound X, then an elimination reaction to form compound Y and finally
          the polymerisation of Y to form PVC, as outlined in the reaction scheme above.

                 (i)    State the systematic IUPAC name for compound X.

                  (ii)  Name the inorganic compound that is eliminated when X is converted to Y.

                  (iii)  Draw the structure of a molecule of X showing all of its atoms and all of its bonds and
                 indicate clearly which of its bonds are broken during the elimination reaction.

               (iv)  Draw the structure of a molecule of Y showing all of its atoms and all of its bonds and
                 indicate clearly which of its bonds are formed during the elimination reaction.

             (v)  Draw two repeating units of the polymer PVC.                                     (24)
