                                               colourless       purple       colourless
               (i)   Explain the underlined term.                                                   (7)

              compare with the colour of the original mixture of hydrogen and iodine?      (3)

             ‘When a stress is applied to a system in chemical equilibrium, reaction will

                 of adding more hydrogen.                                                      (9)

          chemical reaction of iron to generate heat. When
