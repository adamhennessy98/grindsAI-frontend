Section B
At least five questions must be answered from this section.
Eight items to be answered in Question 4. Six marks allocated to each item and one additional mark to be
added to each of the first two items for which the highest marks are awarded.

candidates who attempt Question 11 are required to answer two of the parts (a), (b) and (c) where
candidates who answer part (c) may choose A or B.
