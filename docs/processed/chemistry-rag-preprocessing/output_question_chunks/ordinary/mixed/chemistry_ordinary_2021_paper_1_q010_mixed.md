# Question

8.     Study the reaction scheme and answer the questions that follow.


             1,2-dibromoethane            ethanol                chloroethane

                           A               Al2O3        B

                  X                   ethene                polymer Y
                              + H2



      (a)    (i)   Name the homologous series to which ethene (C2H4) belongs.
                  (ii)  Draw the structure of an ethene molecule showing all of its atoms and bonds.
                  (iii)   Is the geometry around the carbon atoms in ethene planar or tetrahedral?  (15)

      (b)    (i)   Name the product X of the addition of H2 to ethene.
                  (ii)  A and B are two other addition reactions of ethene.
                  Identify the substance added to ethene in each case.                       (12)

       (c)  Name the reaction type involved when ethanol is converted to ethene
           using heated Al2O3.                                                                     (6)

      (d)  Name Y which is made by polymerising ethene.                                       (6)

       (e)   Benzene, an aromatic compound, is unreactive with regard to addition reactions.
                 (i)    Explain the underlined term.
                  (ii)  Draw the structure of a benzene molecule.                                 (11)
