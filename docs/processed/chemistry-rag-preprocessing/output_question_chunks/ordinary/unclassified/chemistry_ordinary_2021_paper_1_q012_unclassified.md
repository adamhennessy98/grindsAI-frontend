# Question

10.  Answer any two of the parts (a), (b) and (c).                                        (2 × 25)

      (a)  The following words are omitted from the passage below.

           neutral            Rutherford        atomic                 positive
                   Bohr             mass             negative
          Write in your answerbook the omitted word corresponding to each of the
         numbers (1 to 7).

         The number of protons in an atom is its  1  number and the sum of the protons and
          neutrons in its nucleus is its  2  number. The number of electrons in the electron
           cloud of a  3  atom is the same as the number of protons in its nucleus.
                It was  4  who discovered that an atom has a small dense  5  central nucleus
         and it was  6  who first described the arrangement of the electrons in energy levels
             in the electron cloud of an atom. In a chemical reaction an atom becomes charged if it
            loses one or more of its electrons or gains one or more additional electrons.  If the
         atom gains one or more electrons it becomes a  7  ion.                         (25)

      (b)  The diagram shows how acidified water can be electrolysed           O2        H2
           using inert electrodes. The overall reaction that takes
           place during the electrolysis is:

                 H2O (l) → H2 (g) + ½O2 (g)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          (anode)                                                                                         (cathode)
            This is a redox reaction.                 X         Y

           Define (i) oxidation, (ii) reduction, in terms of electron transfer.

                  (iii)  What is the ratio, by volume, of H2 to O2 produced?

               (iv)   Identify a suitable material for use as the inert electrodes.

             (v)   At which electrode, X or Y, does oxidation take place?                      (25)


       (c)   AdBlue, a solution of urea (NH2CONH2) in
           water, is used in the exhaust systems of some
          diesel-powered vehicles to remove NO2
          from the hot exhaust flow by making use of
          the following reaction.

                                     catalyst
         4NH2CONH2 + 6NO2 →  7N2 + 8H2O + 4CO2
                             400 ⁰C

                 (i)   What is a catalyst?
                  (ii)  What is the mass of one mole of NH2CONH2?
               There are 1,620 g of urea in a container of AdBlue.
           How many moles of urea are there in the container?
                  (iii)  How many moles of NO2 gas react with one mole of urea in the reaction above?
             What volume does this amount of NO2 occupy, measured at s.t.p.?         (25)
