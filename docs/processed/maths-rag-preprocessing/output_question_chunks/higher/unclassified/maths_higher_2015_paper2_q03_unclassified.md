Section A                  Concepts and Skills                 150 marks

Question 3                                                                      (25 marks)
(a)  The co-ordinates of two points are A(4, − 1) and B (7, t ).

     The line l1 :3 x − 4 y − 12 = 0 is perpendicular to AB. Find the value of t.





(b)  Find, in terms of k, the distance between the point P (10, k ) and 1l .





(c)  P (10, k ) is on a bisector of the angles between the lines 1l and l 2 :5 x + 12 y − 20 = 0.
        (i)   Find the possible values of k.





        (ii)   If k > 0, find the distance from P to 1l .
