(a)  A group of students decided to investigate the average amount of time that students in
      Ireland spend reading each day.
    One Monday morning, they asked the first 200 students who came into their school how
      long, on average, they spend reading each day. Their results are shown in the table below.

 Minutes spent
                    0 −30   30 −60   60 −90  90 −120 120 −150 150 −180
 reading per day

 Number of students     16       35       62       59       18       10

Note: 30 −60 means “30 minutes or more, but less than 60 minutes”, and so on.

        (i)   Work out the maximum number of students in this sample who, on average, could
          have read for more than 100 minutes per day.





        (ii)   Estimate the median time spent reading by these 200 students, as accurately as
            possible. Justify your answer.





         (iii)  Explain why the method of choosing the students for this survey could limit the validity
           of the conclusions.
